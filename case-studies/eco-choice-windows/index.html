The Thesis
Most agencies would have taken the ad budget and called it a day. The ads are the last thing we built.
The Brief

The objective, and what stood in the way.

The Objective

Turn a Vaughan-based windows and doors manufacturer into a predictable growth engine. Qualified quote requests week in, week out, with every dollar accountable from first click to closed install. Not more traffic. More installs.

The Challenge

The category is savage. A considered, high-ticket purchase with a long decision cycle, seasonal demand, and an auction flooded with discount messaging. The inherited account was flat: broad keywords, one-size ad copy, repair-intent clicks burning budget, and no way to tell which ad actually produced a quote. Traffic without a conversion system would have burned the budget faster.

The Bet

Build the whole engine before scaling spend. Understand the buyer first, rebuild the accounts around intent, give every click a page built to convert, and wire attribution through to the CRM so the results are facts, not feelings.

The North Star · Set Before Launch

Hard projections modeled on Eco Choice's first-party data and industry benchmarks, signed off before a dollar of media ran. Everyone knew the target. Everyone knows the score.

The Build

Four layers, one scoreboard.

01. Pages

Designed and built the conversion pages first: offer-led landing pages with quote flows engineered for a high-ticket considered purchase, not a generic contact form.

02. Creative

Produced the ad system in-house: static offer ads, UGC-style video concepts, and carousel formats, each angle mapped to a real buying trigger. Comfort, energy cost, curb appeal, financing.

03. CRO

Live experimentation on the quote flow from day one, run through Zoho PageSense. Every batch of creative feeds learning back into the pages, and every page test feeds back into the ads.

04. CRM

Every quote request lands in Zoho CRM with its source attached. Speed-to-lead automations fire in minutes, not days, and closed revenue ties back to the exact ad that started it. That is the scoreboard everything else answers to.

The Search Rebuild

Google, rebuilt from intent up.

01. Audit

We inherited a flat account: broad keywords, one-size ad copy, and repair-intent traffic quietly burning budget on a company that sells replacements.

02. Search, by intent

Ad groups split by what the searcher actually wants: window replacement, door replacement, tilt and turn, brand. Headlines pinned so the offer never rotates out of position one.

03. Performance Max

PMax runs on curated asset groups built from the Meta-proven creative, so Google's machine learns from hooks that already won, not stock guesses.

04. The negative wall

Repair, parts, DIY, rental. Blocked before they spend a dollar. Then every search click lands on the angle-matched dynamic page you'll see below.

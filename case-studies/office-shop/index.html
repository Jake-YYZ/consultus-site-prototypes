The Brief

Invisible in new markets.

About The Office Shop

The Office Shop is an independently owned Canadian office furniture supplier that has helped design and furnish offices across the country for more than 25 years.

The Challenge

The Office Shop wanted to grow beyond its home market and reach buyers searching for office furniture in cities across Canada. Almost every one of those buyers starts on Google, but the site was not showing up for the high-intent local searches that lead to sales. They needed organic visibility in market after market, not just at home.

The Goal · Own Local Search
Enter New MarketsRank for office-furniture searches in target cities beyond the home base.
Grow Organic TrafficTurn higher search visibility into real clicks and inbound leads.
Page-One RankingsLand on the first page of Google for the keywords that matter most.
Build ✓Optimize ✓Rank ✓
How We Did It

A search program built to rank.

01. Website Development

We started with the foundation, developing the site so it was structured, crawlable, and ready to support a serious SEO program across many markets.

02. Location Pages for Targeted Cities

We built dedicated, optimized pages for each target city so The Office Shop could rank for local searches in every market it wanted to enter, not just its home base.

03. On-Page SEO Optimizations

Titles, headings, metadata, and content were tuned around the keywords buyers actually search, both on-page and off-page, to signal relevance to Google in each city.

04. Strategic Backlinks

We earned quality backlinks to build the site’s authority, giving those location pages the off-page strength they needed to climb into page-one positions.

05. Website Performance Improvements

Speed and technical performance upgrades made the site faster and healthier, improving both the user experience and how search engines ranked it.

The Brief

Eighty years of reputation, stuck in 2012.

About Western Asphalt

Western Asphalt is a leading B2B service provider in the asphalt industry, serving high-profile clients such as governments, airports, and port authorities. With a history spanning more than 80 years, it had built a strong reputation, but its digital presence no longer matched it.

The Challenge

Western Asphalt needed a website that was both visually compelling and technically sound: a modern design to lift brand perception, a mobile-optimized experience, a backend the internal team could update themselves, a strong SEO foundation, and richer engagement through visual storytelling and video.

How We Did It

A rebuild in three parts.

01. Design & UX Enhancements

Consistent, professional design across every page, strategic call-to-action placement to drive conversions, and a unified layout and navigation with a clear, semantic flow from top-level pages down to subpages, so browsing is intuitive.

02. Technical Upgrades

Built on WordPress so the internal team can manage updates easily. Strong caching, asset compression, and optimized hosting keep the media-rich pages fast, on a clean codebase with an optimized structure and strategic metadata for search visibility.

03. Visual Identity & Branding

We used the existing brand kit to keep things consistent, then integrated custom photography and videography to add authenticity and professional polish, optimized to showcase the true scale and capabilities of the company.

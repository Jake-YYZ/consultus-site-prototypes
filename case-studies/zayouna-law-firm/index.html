The Brief

The objective, and what stood in the way.

The Objective

Turn a five-year marketing relationship into a file-generating machine. Not impressions, not even leads: signed personal injury and litigation cases, attributable to the ad that started them.

The Challenge

Toronto personal injury is a brutal auction dominated by billboard firms and settlement mills. Benchmark cost per lead sits at $442. And the painful internal truth: most ad leads weren't sales-qualified, so volume alone was vanity.

The Bet

Creative is the new targeting. Persona-built ads find the right injured person; a dynamic landing page greets them with their own story; and the intake CRM gets audited and rebuilt so good leads become signed files instead of voicemail.

The Landing Page

One page that shapeshifts.

01. Dynamic by design

One landing page at zaylaw.com/lp. The ad you clicked decides what you see: motor vehicle content for the MVA ad, family content for the family ad, slip-and-fall content in the winter. The visitor always lands mid-conversation.

02. Wired to intake

Every form fill flows straight into the firm's intake CRM with its source, campaign, and creative attached. No re-keying, no lost context, no mystery leads.

03. Built from the audit

We audited the firm's entire intake process first: eleven lead statuses, custom fields per case type, the automation gaps, the scripts. The landing page is one output of that rev ops work, not a standalone art project.

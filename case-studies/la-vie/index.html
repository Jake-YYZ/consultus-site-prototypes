The Brief

The objective, and what stood in the way.

The Objective

Take a beloved Ottawa clinic into Oakville, one of Canada's most competitive private-health markets, and fill the new clinic with the right patients: people ready to invest in a two-hour physician assessment instead of a fifteen-minute rush.

The Challenge

In Ottawa, people search for La Vie by name. In Oakville, nobody knew the name, searches were service-led, costs per click were higher, and giants like Medcan and Cleveland Clinic Canada own the category's mindshare. Awareness had to be built, not bought cheaply.

The Bet

Premium, calm, authority-driven creative, no fear tactics, no biohacker buzzwords. Google captures the intent that exists, Meta builds the awareness that doesn't, LinkedIn reaches the executives, and every lead lands in the CRM with its source attached.

The Build

Five layers, one scoreboard.

01. Creative

A production day at the clinic: six video concepts with A/B hooks (36 masters), physician-to-camera and patient testimonials, plus a static system across comparison, pull-quote, testimonial, statement, and lifestyle families.

02. Paid media

Google captures Oakville's service-led searches. Meta builds the awareness the market lacks. LinkedIn reaches senior decision-makers. Each channel has a job, all answer to one number.

03. The landing page

A dedicated Oakville conversion page with the clinic tour video up top and a lead flow wired straight into the CRM.

04. Search foundation

SEO and AI-search optimization from day one: ten content pages and six high-authority backlinks in month one, 23 keywords ranking top-3 in Oakville, and 5,000 organic sessions at 92% engagement.

05. CRM and attribution

Lead routing workflows, paid-social attribution fixed at the source, and every inquiry tagged by channel and creative. 172 organic calls and 136 paid calls in May, and we can tell you which is which.

The Brief

The objective, and what stood in the way.

The Objective

Reposition a 68-year-old family company from "the downsizing people" to a Real Estate Plus brokerage, the one team that handles the sale, the sorting, the move, and everything between. Then build the growth engine that reputation deserves.

The Challenge

The audience is seniors and their adult children, in one of life's hardest transitions. Trust is everything, attention is fragile, and every ad must carry RECO brokerage compliance. The old positioning under-credited the real estate side of the business entirely.

The Bet

Reposition first, then advertise. Lead with real people and real reviews instead of property glamour shots. And wire every lead back to its source, so the family stories and the spreadsheet finally agree.

The Build

Five layers, one roof.

01. Website

Full WordPress rebuild of gogordons.com: the Real Estate Plus story, a listings system with sold data, accessibility and privacy done properly, and a trained-up realtor team with their own manual and webinar.

02. Landing page

A dedicated conversion page at landing.gogordons.com for paid traffic, built for one job: turn a worried family's click into a conversation.

03. Creative

Creator-produced UGC mapped to the two personas, plus statics and motion graphics. Real voices over property glamour, every cut RECO-compliant.

04. Paid media

Meta with Pixel and Conversions API wired for real signal, UGC-first campaigns built broad for Ontario. Google structured around trigger events: probate, estate planning, mobility changes, and the "near me" searches that convert best.

05. Search foundation

SEO, AEO, and GEO content including service-area pages written in Gordon's voice, so executors searching "estate settlement Toronto" find the people who actually do it.

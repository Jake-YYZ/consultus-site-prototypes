The Brief

The objective, and what stood in the way.

The Objective

Make BookSeats the go-to for sports travel packages, and scale ticket-package sales profitably. Not by outbidding Ticketmaster and Vivid Seats on generic ticket searches, but by owning the niche they can't: the whole trip, bundled.

The Challenge

A young brand in a category where trust is everything. Existing creative was broker-style: stock images, generic offers, nothing that felt native in a feed. Paid traffic existed, but ROAS hovered around 1.4x and every audience leaked efficiency.

The Bet

Real fans beat stock photos. A UGC-first creative engine, league-specific funnels instead of one homepage, and a kill-fast testing culture. Then let the scoreboard decide.

The Search Calendar

Google, played like a season ticket.

01. Partner-team campaigns

Dedicated campaigns for BookSeats' official team partnerships: Packers, Saints, Texans, Wild, Golden Knights, Hurricanes, Utes. Co-branded inventory, team-specific copy, fan-specific intent.

02. Season and playoff surges

Campaigns timed to the sports calendar: NFL kickoff, NHL playoffs, NBA runs, NCAA tournaments, holiday gifting. Budgets surge exactly when fans start planning trips, and rest when they don't.

03. The niche play

We never entered the ticket-only arms race against Ticketmaster and Vivid Seats. The account owns one thing: high-intent sports travel package searches, where the bundle is the advantage.

The Method

Our CRO process.

  1. 01

    Baseline and Benchmark

    Before we can make any recommendations, we need to get to know your business, your goals, your challenges, and the digital marketing tools you are currently using. Our team will also do an analysis to see how your business’s current conversion rate compares to industry and direct competitor benchmarks.

  2. 02

    Analytics Audit

    Once we have the basics down, we will do an even deeper analysis known as an analytics audit.

    This process entails looking at your analytics and analyzing your:

    • Website
    • Landing page
    • Session time
    • Bounce rate
    • Referral channel
    • Web design

    By looking at these elements, our goal is to try and identify why your conversion rate may be lower than it should be.

  3. 03

    Roadmap to Convert

    After we have confirmed why your conversion rate is low, we’ll get to work on creating a customized CRO strategy that includes the following digital marketing tools:

    • Web design
    • Content marketing
    • SEO
    • PPC
    • And more
  4. 04

    CRO Implementation

    The next step is to put our plan into action through the optimization of your website and landing pages. This includes improving:

    • CTAs
    • Page load speed
    • Content quality
    • And more

    We’ll also conduct A/B testing to see what is working and what isn’t so we can make adjustments.

  5. 05

    Evaluate and Optimize

    Our CRO strategies aren’t designed to be quick fixes. With any work we do, we continuously test and refine things to make sure you are achieving desirable results.

FAQ

The questions Vancouver businesses actually ask.

How much should I spend on Conversion Rate Optimization in Vancouver?

Most Vancouver engagements we run sit between $3,000 and $20,000 per month, with outliers in either direction. The right number depends on your average customer lifetime value, target cost per acquisition, and how saturated your category is in the Vancouver market. Before we recommend a budget, we audit historical performance, current creative or content quality, and your conversion rate. Spending without that baseline is gambling, not media buying.

How long until I see results from Conversion Rate Optimization?

Most CRO engagements show meaningful signal within 14 to 28 days, with stable predictable behaviour usually settling between days 45 and 90. Anyone promising day-one outcomes on cold accounts is exaggerating. SEO is the slowest of the family. Count six months for compounding results.

Do you take over my existing account or start a new one?

In most cases we take over the existing account. Historical performance data, audience signals, and account history all carry forward, which is valuable. If your account has structural issues that cannot be fixed in place, we recommend a new build alongside the legacy account so we do not lose history.

What industries in Vancouver do you specialize in?

Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. The specialization matters because Vancouver buying behaviour and competitor density differ from other markets. We tune the approach to how Vancouver buyers actually behave, not to a national playbook.

How is your reporting different from other CRO agencies?

Our standard monthly report ties every dollar of spend to a tracked outcome (lead, booking, or revenue event), broken down by campaign, channel, and audience. Most agency reports stop at impressions, clicks, and CTR. We start there and keep going, because the conversation that matters is "did this work" not "did the program run."

What does an engagement with Consultus Digital actually look like in month one?

Week one is access plus audit. Week two we lock strategy and budget. Week three we build. Week four we launch and measure. By the end of month one you have a working program against your KPIs and a baseline report. Nothing exotic, just disciplined.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. We work on month-to-month retainers. Annual commitments are available at a discount but they are optional. If our work isn't creating results, you shouldn't be locked in.

Are you a certified partner with the major platforms?

Yes. We are a certified Google Partner, Meta Business Partner, Microsoft Advertising Partner, and Zoho Authorized Partner. Partner status alone doesn't guarantee a good agency, but its absence is a fair signal that an agency may not have invested in the credentials of its team.

FAQ

Calgary questions, answered.

Do you actually work with Calgary businesses, and do you have a local team?
Yes. Consultus Digital runs active campaigns for clients across the Calgary metro. We are headquartered in Toronto with a senior, remote-capable team, so we do not operate a Calgary storefront, and we are upfront about that. What you get instead is specialist depth: experienced strategists and channel experts dedicated to your account. We learn the Calgary market, your seasonality, and your competitive set, and most of our client communication happens over calls, screen-shares, and reporting anyway. You get real expertise serving Calgary, not a token local office padding your invoice.
Which services should my Calgary business start with?
It depends on your goals and timeline. If you need leads quickly, paid channels like Google Ads and Meta deliver fastest in the Calgary market. If you want durable, lower-cost visibility over time, SEO and content are the long game. Most Calgary clients run a mix, with CRO and CRM underneath to convert and track everything. We do not push a one-size package. Consultus Digital audits your situation, your competition, and your numbers first, then recommends the channel mix that gets you the best return on your budget.
How does your pricing and billing work?
Consultus Digital works on a monthly retainer scoped to your goals, channels, and the level of management your Calgary campaigns require. Pricing depends on which services you run and your ad budget, so we quote after a discovery conversation instead of posting a generic number that wouldn't fit your business. Ad spend is separate and goes directly to the platforms; you keep it in your own accounts. There are no hidden fees. You will know exactly what you're paying for and what return we're working toward before anything starts.
How long until I see results?
Paid channels move fastest. Google Ads and Meta campaigns can start driving leads within the first weeks, then improve as we gather data and optimize, with meaningful traction typically over the first few months. SEO is a longer investment that usually shows real momentum over several months and compounds from there. CRO improvements often deliver some of the quickest wins because they lift conversion on traffic you already have. We set clear expectations for your Calgary program up front so you know what to expect and when, by channel.
What reporting and transparency do I get?
You get clear, regular reporting in plain English, with full access to your own ad and analytics accounts at all times. Consultus Digital never locks Calgary clients out of their own data. We show you what we're doing, what it is producing, and what we're changing next, focused on the metrics that matter: qualified leads, cost per lead, and revenue. If a campaign is underperforming, you'll hear it from us straight, with a plan to fix it. Transparency isn't a feature we add on, it is how we operate.
What industries do you have experience with?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also work across other verticals that drive the Calgary economy, including real estate and energy. We have built campaigns for service businesses chasing local leads, B2B firms reaching sophisticated buyers, and consumer brands focused on return on ad spend. The playbook adapts to your industry and your Calgary buyers. If your business depends on generating qualified leads or profitable sales online, there is a strong chance we've run something close to it before.
What makes you different from other Calgary agencies?
Start with who does the work. Your account is run by senior in-house specialists, not junior staff and not outsourced labour, and they treat the budget the way they would treat their own. Everything else follows from that. We optimize for revenue and qualified leads, which is how Calgary buyers tend to think anyway. The full funnel sits under one roof, from ads through landing pages and CRO to CRM and attribution, so a lead converts and gets tracked all the way to revenue. Reporting is transparent and the accounts stay yours.
Do you handle both B2B and B2C marketing?
Yes. Consultus Digital runs both, and Calgary needs both. On the B2C side, we drive leads and sales for home services, real estate, healthcare, and consumer brands with high-intent search and scroll-stopping social. On the B2B side, we reach the sophisticated decision-makers common in Calgary's energy and professional services sectors with credibility-led search, content, and account-focused campaigns. The strategy, creative, and sales-cycle length differ, but the discipline is the same: understand the buyer, earn the click, convert it, and prove the return.
How do we get started?
It starts with a strategy call. We learn your Calgary business, your goals, your current marketing, and your numbers, then audit where the biggest opportunities are. From there, Consultus Digital builds a recommended plan and scope, and once you approve it, onboarding is fast. We will need access to your ad accounts, analytics, and any existing assets, and we handle the technical setup for you. In the first 30 days we get tracking right, launch or restructure campaigns, and establish the reporting baseline so progress is clear from day one.

Our process

The Consultus Digital Method.

Our creative offering is built around harnessing your brand to tell a good story. Through video and photography we help you showcase products, share customer testimonials, rank for tutorial searches, and drive engagement, with an authentic brand experience behind all of it.

Exploration

We sit down with your team to understand your specific creative and marketing goals, then develop a strategy that fulfills them.
Whether it’s a product launch, social content, customer feedback, or an influencer campaign, we get a clear picture of what you actually need before anyone picks up a camera.

Storyboarding

Our team creates storyboards for every piece to be produced, and identifies how to tailor each one per platform.
We prepare a detailed script with directions for each shot, dialogue or text, graphics, and b-roll, and plan for a productive shoot rather than a hopeful one.

Capture

Once you’ve approved the storyboards and scripts, we shoot.
The team arrives fully equipped for every shoot planned: professional cameras, lighting, and audio, along with the sets, materials, and on- or off-camera talent the script calls for.

Post and distribution

After the shoot we move to editing and post-production on each asset.
We splice content for highlight reels, social posts, and your landing pages, and upload full-length and behind-the-scenes cuts across your channels. Combined with video SEO, social, and YouTube advertising, every asset reaches the audience it was made for.

Video testimonials

Let your customers make the argument.

A testimonial from a real customer does something your own copy cannot, however well written. It is the difference between a claim and a demonstration.

The formats

  • Customer or client testimonials. The core format: a real buyer describing the problem they had and what changed.
  • Employee testimonials. Powerful for recruitment and for professional services, where the team is the product.
  • Remote testimonials. Captured wherever the customer is, which makes it practical to feature clients outside your city.
  • On-site testimonials. Filmed at the customer’s premises, which grounds the story in a real place and usually makes it more specific.

Why they work

  1. Credibility and trust. Customer testimonials carry proof your own marketing cannot manufacture, which is exactly why buyers seek them out.
  2. Increased referrals. A customer who has articulated why they like you on camera tends to keep saying it off camera too.
  3. Instant shares and engagement. Testimonial video travels, because the person featured has their own reason to share it.
  4. Repurpose the content. One good testimonial shoot yields ad cuts, landing page embeds, social posts, and sales collateral.

Distribution

Creating it is just the first step.

The real power of production is in how it integrates with the rest of your marketing. Buyers want more video from the businesses they buy from, it keeps visitors on your site longer, and it gives an email something worth clicking.

Organic / 01

SEO

Use video to rank on product, how-to, and brand searches. It complements copy-based SEO, letting you rank through more than one content format on the same query.

Display / 02

Banner and social ads

Instead of a static graphic, run short-form video in display placements and social feeds. It drives more clicks through to your site and strengthens the conversion path behind them.

Video / 03

Pre, mid, and post-roll

Play your ad before, during, or after an online video on platforms like YouTube. You pay for the ad type you select, whether that is a few seconds of viewing or a completed view, and reach customers at any point in the journey.

The advantages

What are the benefits of custom photography?

Everyone has a camera in their pocket, which is exactly why the gap between adequate and good has become so visible. It takes a skilled eye to bring the pieces together into something memorable.

01 / Quality

Professional photos

Properly lit, properly composed, properly retouched. The difference reads instantly to a customer even when they could not tell you what changed, and it is the cheapest credibility a brand can buy.

02 / Consistency

A cohesive, memorable aesthetic

A shared treatment across every image means your work is recognizable before anyone reads the logo. That consistency is what separates a brand from a collection of assets.

03 / Reuse

One visual identity, endless uses

A single shoot feeds your website, ads, social, email, print, and sales collateral. Custom photography is one of the few investments where the asset library keeps paying out long after the invoice.

04 / Story

Brand storytelling

Images carry the parts of your story that copy struggles with: the craft, the people, the place, the care. Those are the details that turn a considered purchase into an easy one.

How we work

What you get working with our creative team.

Everything in-house

Strategy, scripting, shooting, editing, and design all sit under one roof. That is how we hold quality steady and hit tight deadlines, and why a change of direction mid-campaign doesn’t become a three-week delay.

Made to be tested

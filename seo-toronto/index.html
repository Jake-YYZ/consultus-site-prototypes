The Practice

Toronto's SEO and GEO agency.

SEO, then GEO, working together.

Search Engine Optimization is the process of convincing Google's search engine, and your target audience, that your website is the most relevant result to show for a given query. The websites that earn those positions are called organic, natural, or earned results. They have not paid Google to appear. That earned authority is exactly what makes SEO one of the highest-ROI marketing investments a Toronto business can make.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the newer discipline that sits alongside traditional SEO. It is the practice of structuring your content and brand presence so that AI-powered search tools like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini cite your business in the answers they generate. In 2026, these two disciplines are inseparable. More than 57% of all Google search results pages now include an AI-generated answer above the organic results, and organic click-through rates have dropped 61% on queries where those AI Overviews appear. Your business needs to be visible in both environments simultaneously.

The numbers behind the channel.

Over 70% of global search traffic happens on Google. Of that, 67.6% of all clicks go to the first five organic results. But in 2026, the businesses that are only chasing organic positions are missing the fastest-growing source of high-intent traffic: AI citations. Traffic referred from AI platforms converts at up to 23 times the rate of standard organic search traffic, because those visitors have already been vetted and recommended by the AI before they arrive at your site.

What is SEO in 2026?

Earned visibility, on every surface that matters.

Earned, not paid.

Search Engine Optimization is the process of earning organic visibility on Google and other search engines by demonstrating to those platforms that your website is the most relevant, authoritative, and trustworthy result for the queries your customers are typing.

The websites that appear organically on Google's results page have not paid for those positions. They have earned them through a combination of technical website quality, relevant and authoritative content, credible backlinks from other trusted sites, and a positive user experience. The advantage of this earned visibility is compounding: once you hold strong rankings, the traffic continues to arrive without paying for every click the way you would with Google Ads.

How AI Overviews changed the game.

In 2026, SEO also encompasses everything that positions your business to be cited by AI-powered search tools. Google AI Overviews, which now appear in more than 57% of search results pages, draw 99% of their citations from pages already in Google's organic top ten. Your traditional SEO investment is the direct prerequisite for AI Overview visibility. Strong rankings are the foundation. GEO is the layer built on top of that foundation to capture the AI search opportunity.

FAQ

Common questions from Toronto buyers.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term investment that typically takes six to twelve months to reach its full potential. Technical fixes show in Search Console within weeks; content authority and link earning compound across quarters. GEO visibility compounds on a similar timeline.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO optimizes for traditional search engine results pages on Google and Bing. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes for citations and answers within AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini. They share fundamentals (technical health, content authority, schema), but the success metrics and content patterns differ. We run them as parallel programs because Google rankings are the foundation AI citations are built on.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes. While our SEO programs are tuned for Toronto-area buyers, we run engagements across North America. Our city-specific SEO landing pages exist for clients who want a local market focus, and our national SEO program is documented separately.

Will my business be visible in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?

That is exactly what GEO is designed to achieve. Through schema implementation, answer-first content structure, AI crawler access (PerplexityBot, OAI-SearchBot, GoogleOther), and entity-level brand clarity, we position your business to be cited in generative answers across the major AI platforms.

Do you do AI-generated content?

We use AI for research, outline structure, and keyword network mapping. Final copy is written and edited by humans with subject-matter input. Google's helpful-content systems are designed to demote pure AI output and they continue to improve at it.

Can you work with our existing in-house SEO or content team?

Yes. Many of our best engagements are co-managed. We bring strategy, technical work, schema implementation, and the GEO discipline while your in-house team owns content production. We define the briefs, the schema spec, and the success metrics.

How do I choose the best SEO company in Toronto?

Look past the rankings a firm claims for its own name and ask three things. First, do they measure success in Search Console and organic revenue, or in vanity keyword positions? Second, do they earn links through digital PR rather than buying them, which is what eventually gets a site penalized? Third, in 2026, do they do GEO as well as SEO? A good Toronto SEO firm should be able to show you real client results and explain exactly what they would do in your first 90 days. If an SEO company promises page-one rankings in 30 days, walk away.

Do you offer managed SEO services?

Yes. Our managed SEO services are the default engagement: we own the strategy, technical work, content briefs, link earning, schema, and the GEO layer as an ongoing monthly program, and report on it in a shared dashboard. You get a senior team running the whole discipline rather than a junior contractor and a checklist. For teams that only need part of the program, we also co-manage alongside an in-house SEO lead.

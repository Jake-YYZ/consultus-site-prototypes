Why Vancouver

Growth in Vancouver, built on a scoreboard.

Every program we run in Vancouver is measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. See the results we have driven for businesses like you.

Vancouver is one of the most expensive, most design-literate markets in North America, and that shapes how buyers behave. Real estate, technology, tourism and hospitality, wellness, and film anchor the local economy, and customers here have high expectations for how a brand presents itself. A cheap-looking ad or a clunky landing page gets dismissed in seconds. That raises the bar for marketing in Vancouver: you can't win on volume alone, because the audience is discerning, the cost of attention is high, and a polished competitor is always one scroll away.

It is also a competitive market with real structural quirks. Vancouver real estate marketing is governed by tight regulation and brutal cost-per-lead economics. The tech corridor stretching from downtown to Mount Pleasant and out to Burnaby is crowded with venture-backed brands fighting for the same talent and customers. Tourism and hospitality swing hard with the seasons, and wellness and film bring their own buyer psychology. A generic, copy-paste campaign that ignores these dynamics burns budget fast. Winning in Vancouver means understanding which channels actually drive pipeline for your specific vertical, then engineering creative and offers that match the local standard.

Consultus Digital approaches Vancouver the way the city's best businesses run their own businesses: premium execution, zero tolerance for waste. We build campaigns that look as sharp as the market demands, then wire every lead from the first ad click through to a closed deal in your CRM. That means you see exactly which channels, creatives, and pages produce revenue, and which to cut. We are headquartered in Toronto, with senior specialists running active campaigns for Vancouver clients remotely, so you get the same standard of work and a partner who treats your scoreboard like their own.

FAQ

Vancouver questions, answered.

Do you have a team in Vancouver?
We are headquartered in Toronto, with senior specialists running active campaigns for Vancouver clients. We are honest about this: it is a remote-capable senior team, not a storefront on Robson Street. What you get is experienced strategists on your account from day one, the same standard of work we bring to our home market, and a partner who treats your Vancouver revenue scoreboard like their own. Most of our clients never see the inside of an office anyway. They see results, clear reporting, and a team that answers fast.
Which services should a Vancouver business start with?
It depends on your goals and timeline. If you need leads or sales quickly, paid media, Google Ads and Meta Ads, usually moves first. If you want a compounding, lower-cost channel over time, SEO and AEO are the long game. Most Vancouver businesses do best with a full-funnel mix, because ads, pages, and follow-up reinforce each other. We start with a strategy call and an audit, then recommend the smallest mix that hits your goal. We do not sell you channels you do not need.
How much does digital marketing in Vancouver cost?
There is no single number, because a local wellness studio and a venture-backed tech brand have very different needs. Your investment depends on the channels, the competitiveness of your vertical, and your growth targets, and ad budget is separate from management fees. Vancouver is a premium-cost market, so we're upfront about what it takes to compete in your space. After a strategy call we give you a clear, plain-English scope and price with no hidden retainers. You always know what you're paying for and what it should produce.
How long until I see results?
Paid channels move first. With Google Ads and Meta Ads you can see qualified leads within the first few weeks, then we optimize from there as data comes in. SEO is a longer game, usually several months to build the authority and rankings that compound into your lowest-cost channel. We set realistic timelines up front so there are no surprises. In a market as competitive as Vancouver, anyone promising instant top rankings isn't being straight with you.
What reporting and transparency do I get?
You own your ad and analytics accounts, always, so nothing is locked in a black box. You get clear reporting in plain English, on a regular cadence, tied to the metrics that matter: leads, revenue, and true cost per customer, not vanity numbers. Because we connect your CRM, you can trace a closed deal back to the exact channel, campaign, and creative that produced it. If something is underperforming, we flag it and tell you what we're doing about it. Transparency is the whole model, not a feature.
Which industries do you work with in Vancouver?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also run programs across other parts of Vancouver's core economy, including real estate, technology and SaaS, tourism and hospitality, and film and creative, along with B2B brands. The channel playbook adapts to your vertical and its buyer psychology, but the accountability never changes. If you aren't sure whether we've worked with a business like yours, ask on the strategy call and we'll be direct about our fit.
What makes Consultus Digital different from other Vancouver agencies?
Every dollar gets measured against pipeline, so revenue is the number we report on. The rest of it is plumbing: ads, SEO, creative, web, CRO and CRM under one roof, so your leads convert instead of leaking out of a broken handoff. You own your accounts. You get reporting in plain English. You see the same scoreboard we do. Vancouver expects polish and we deliver it, then back it with accountability that survives a hard question.
Do you handle both B2B and B2C in Vancouver?
Yes. We run B2C programs for local Vancouver businesses like wellness clinics, real estate, hospitality, and ecommerce, where speed, creative, and conversion drive the result. We also run B2B demand generation for the city's tech and professional services brands, where the focus is qualified pipeline, longer sales cycles, and tight CRM attribution. The strategy and reporting are tuned to which one you are. The constant is that we track every lead to closed revenue, whether your buyer is a consumer or a procurement team.
How do we get started?
Book a strategy call. We learn your goals, your market, and your numbers, then audit your current marketing to find the fastest wins and the biggest leaks. From there we give you a clear, plain-English plan and scope, no obligation. To launch quickly we typically need access to your ad and analytics accounts, your website, and any existing creative or brand assets, and we handle the technical setup for you. Your first thirty days are about getting tracking right and putting points on the board, not a six-month runway before anything happens.

Why New York City

Growth in New York City, built on a scoreboard.

Every program we run in New York City is measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. See the results we have driven for businesses like you.

New York City is the financial and commercial capital of the United States, and that gravity shapes everything about marketing here. The metro is home to Wall Street, global real estate developers, white-shoe law firms, hospitality empires, and a DTC and ecommerce scene that sets trends for the rest of the country. Customer acquisition costs in New York City run higher than almost anywhere else because the demand is enormous and so is the competition. A Manhattan med spa, a Brooklyn restaurant group, a B2B SaaS company near the Flatiron District: all of you're bidding against deep-pocketed competitors for the same clicks, the same searches, and the same attention. Generic marketing does not survive that environment.

What makes New York City different is the speed and sophistication of the buyer. People here research fast, compare aggressively, and expect a frictionless experience from the first ad to the final booking. A finance client in the Financial District, a real estate buyer eyeing Long Island City, and a hospitality customer in SoHo all behave differently, and the platforms reward marketers who understand those distinctions. Auction prices on Google Ads and Meta in the New York City market punish sloppy targeting and weak landing pages instantly. The agencies that thrive here are the ones that treat every campaign as a system, where the ad, the page, the offer, and the follow-up are all engineered to convert the specific New York buyer they are speaking to.

Consultus Digital approaches New York City the way a business owner would. We are headquartered in Toronto with senior specialists who run active campaigns in major North American markets, and we work remotely with New York City businesses as a true extension of their team. We do not staff a token storefront in Midtown and call it local expertise. Instead we bring full-funnel discipline to the most demanding ad market on the continent: deep competitor and auction analysis, intent-mapped paid search, conversion-built landing pages, and CRM and attribution so you know exactly which New York City campaigns are producing revenue. When acquisition costs are this high, accountability isn't a nice-to-have. It is the whole point.

FAQ

New York City questions, answered.

Do you work with local New York City businesses if you're based in Toronto?
Yes. Consultus Digital is headquartered in Toronto, and we run active campaigns for businesses across major North American markets, including New York City. We work remotely as a true extension of your team, with senior specialists who know how to compete in the NYC auction environment. Digital marketing doesn't require us to share a zip code with you. It requires deep platform expertise, competitor analysis, and accountability, all of which we deliver from anywhere. We are upfront about this: you get a remote-capable senior team focused on your results, not a token storefront in Midtown.
Which services do New York City businesses usually need from you?
It depends on your goals, and we recommend a mix instead of selling you everything. New York City businesses that need leads or sales quickly usually start with Google Ads and Meta Ads paired with conversion-built landing pages. Those who want durable, lower-cost traffic invest in SEO and AEO over time. Most clients eventually run a full-funnel program: paid media to drive demand, CRO to convert it, and CRM automation to follow up fast. We audit your situation first, then build the channel mix that fits your budget and the competitiveness of your category.
How much does digital marketing in New York City cost?
Pricing depends on scope, the channels involved, and how competitive your category is. New York City is the most expensive ad market on the continent, so media budgets here run higher than in smaller markets, and we're honest about that from the first call. Our management fees are based on the work, not a percentage game that rewards us for spending more of your money. After a discovery call and audit, we give you a clear proposal with the fee, the recommended media budget, and the expected outcomes laid out in plain English. No hidden costs, no surprises.
How long until I see results in the New York City market?
Paid channels move fastest. Google Ads and Meta Ads can start generating leads within the first few weeks, though the New York City market is competitive enough that the first month or two is largely about gathering data and optimizing toward profitable performance. SEO is a longer game, typically several months before you see meaningful organic gains, because you're climbing past entrenched local competitors. We set realistic expectations up front, show you early signals through transparent reporting, and focus on sustainable results, not a quick spike that fades.
What reporting and transparency do I get?
You get clear monthly reporting written in plain English, not a wall of metrics designed to confuse you. We show what we did, what it produced, and what we're doing next, with a focus on leads, revenue, and cost per result, not vanity numbers. Critically, you own your own ad and analytics accounts. We work inside your accounts, so if our relationship ever ends, your data and history stay with you. You can reach your team directly, and you'll never wonder what is happening with your New York City budget.
What industries do you have experience with in New York City?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also work across other verticals that define the New York City economy, including real estate, hospitality and restaurants, and B2B. Our case studies span lead generation, ecommerce, and high-value service businesses, and the discipline transfers directly to the NYC market. We adapt the strategy to your category, because a Manhattan law firm and a Brooklyn DTC brand need very different campaigns. If you're unsure whether we fit, the discovery call will make it clear quickly.
What makes Consultus Digital different from other New York City agencies?
Mostly it comes down to what we agree to be measured on. Consultus Digital optimizes for revenue and qualified leads, which matters enormously when a click in New York City costs what it does. The full funnel sits under one roof, so ads, landing pages, CRO and CRM answer to the same team and a lead does not leak out of a broken handoff. You own your accounts, you get plain-English reporting, and senior people stay on the work. We do not promise number one rankings overnight. We promise a process you can check.
Do you handle both B2B and B2C businesses in New York City?
Yes, and the New York City market has plenty of both. On the B2C side we drive bookings, sales, and foot traffic for restaurants, med spas, ecommerce brands, and real estate. On the B2B side we run intent-driven search, account targeting, and lead nurture for finance, professional services, and SaaS companies, where a single closed client can be worth a great deal. The funnel logic is similar, but the messaging, sales cycle, and channels differ. We tailor the program to whether you're selling to consumers or to other businesses across the five boroughs.
How do we get started with Consultus Digital?

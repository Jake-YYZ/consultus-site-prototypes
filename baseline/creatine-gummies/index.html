02 How to take it

Three gummies.
Once a day.
That's it.

There's no protocol to follow, no window to hit and no cycling off. The only variable that matters is whether you actually take it, which is the problem this format exists to solve.

Step 01

Take three, any time of day

Timing barely moves the needle. Saturation is a running total, so attach it to something you already do without thinking. Coffee works for most people.

Step 02

Give it three to four weeks

That's how long five grams a day takes to fill your stores. You can load with more up front, but you don't need to, and loading is where the stomach complaints come from.

Step 03

Don't stop

Stores drain back down over about a month if you quit. Creatine isn't a cycle, it's a floor, and the whole point is to stay above it.

04 Questions

About the gummies
specifically.

How do you fit five grams into three gummies?

They're larger than a typical vitamin gummy, and the base is built around the creatine rather than the creatine being sprinkled into a candy recipe. That's the trade. Most brands keep the gummy small and cut the dose, which is how you end up needing to chew thirteen of them.

Does the creatine stay stable in a gummy?

Creatine degrades into creatinine in heat and moisture over time, so the formula is pH controlled and the tub is a moisture barrier with a sealed liner. We test for creatinine on every batch and publish the number. Store below 25°C and out of direct sun and they'll hold through the printed date.

Are they vegan?

Yes. They're set with pectin rather than gelatin, and the creatine monohydrate is synthesised, not animal derived.

How much sugar is in a serving?

Six grams across three gummies, which is roughly a third of a small apple. We tested sugar-free versions and they needed sugar alcohols to work, which caused more stomach complaints than the sugar was worth.

Why are they all sour?

Creatine has a faintly bitter edge at five grams. A sour profile covers it far better than sweetness does, so instead of burying the dose we leaned into it. That's also why the acid blend is on the ingredient list rather than a vague "natural flavor" line doing all the work.

What's the guarantee?

Sixty days. If you don't like the flavor or you change your mind, email us and we'll refund it, opened tub included. You don't need to ship anything back.

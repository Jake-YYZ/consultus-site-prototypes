Why Los Angeles

Growth in Los Angeles, built on a scoreboard.

Every program we run in Los Angeles is measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. See the results we have driven for businesses like you.

Los Angeles isn't one market, it is a dozen of them stacked on top of each other. A DTC skincare brand in Culver City, a real estate team chasing listings in the Hollywood Hills, a med spa in Beverly Hills, and a streetwear label out of the Arts District are all competing for attention in the same feed, and they all need completely different playbooks. LA is the second largest metro economy in the United States, anchored by entertainment and media, a deep ecommerce and DTC scene, real estate, and a fast-growing health and wellness sector. The money is here, but so is everyone trying to capture it.

That density is what makes Los Angeles brutal for digital marketing. Cost per click and cost per thousand impressions run higher here than almost anywhere because the auction is packed with well-funded competitors, agencies, and in-house teams who all know what they are doing. A campaign that would print money in a smaller market can quietly bleed budget in LA if the targeting, creative, and landing experience aren't dialed in. Audiences here are also sophisticated and ad-saturated. Angelenos scroll past polished creative all day, so the work has to be sharper, more native, and more relevant to the neighborhood and the buyer than the brand down the block.

Consultus Digital approaches Los Angeles the way a local business owner would, even though our headquarters is in Toronto and our team works remotely with LA clients. We map who you're really up against in your category, build creative that fits how Angelenos actually consume content, and engineer the full funnel from the ad to the landing page to the CRM so leads don't leak. We optimize for revenue and signed business, not impressions you can screenshot. In a market this competitive, that discipline is the difference between buying attention and buying customers.

FAQ

Los Angeles questions, answered.

Do you actually work with Los Angeles businesses if you're based in Toronto?
Yes. Consultus Digital is headquartered in Toronto, and we run active campaigns with a senior, remote-capable team serving Los Angeles clients. We do not operate a fake LA storefront, and we are upfront about that. What matters for performance marketing is the quality of the strategy, media buying, creative, and reporting, all of which we deliver remotely every day. We study your specific LA market, competitors, and neighborhoods, and we communicate clearly so working with us feels like having an extension of your own team, wherever your office sits.
Which services should a Los Angeles business start with?
It depends on your goals and how fast you need results. If you want leads or sales quickly, paid media like Google Ads and Meta usually comes first because it produces traffic right away. If you want durable, compounding growth, SEO and AEO build a foundation that pays off over time. Most LA clients run a blend, with conversion optimization and CRM automation layered in so the traffic actually converts. We start with a discovery call and audit, then recommend the mix that fits your market, budget, and timeline. No cookie-cutter packages.
How much does digital marketing in Los Angeles cost?
There is no flat price because the right investment depends on your goals, your competition, and your channels. Los Angeles is one of the more expensive ad markets in the country, so ad budgets here tend to run higher than in smaller cities for the same volume of results. We typically work on a monthly management model plus your media spend, scaled to what your business can profitably support. After a discovery call and audit we give you a clear, itemized proposal so you know exactly what you're paying for and what return we're aiming to drive. No hidden fees.
How long until I see results?
Paid channels move fastest. Google Ads and Meta campaigns can start generating leads and sales within the first weeks, though it usually takes a few months of optimization to hit efficient, scalable performance in a market as competitive as Los Angeles. SEO and AEO are a longer game, typically several months before you see meaningful ranking and traffic gains, with results compounding from there. We set realistic expectations up front for each channel and report progress along the way, so you always know whether we're on track instead of waiting in the dark.
What reporting and transparency do I get?
You get clear monthly reporting written in plain English, not a wall of metrics designed to impress. We show what we did, what it produced, and what we're doing next, tied to the business outcomes that matter to you. You also own your ad and analytics accounts, so your data is always yours and you can see exactly where every dollar went. We are happy to walk through the numbers on a call any time. Transparency is a core part of how we work, not an add-on.
What industries do you work with in Los Angeles?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also work across other verticals that define the LA economy, including entertainment and media, and real estate. Our approach is built around understanding the buyer in your specific category, then engineering the full funnel to convert them. If you're unsure whether we have experience in your space, ask us on a discovery call. We will be honest about where we can drive the biggest impact for your Los Angeles business.
What makes Consultus Digital different from other LA agencies?
The short answer is that a senior in-house team does the actual work, so nobody hands you off to a junior once the contract is signed. That shapes everything else. We optimize for revenue and qualified leads rather than metrics you cannot bank. Ads, landing pages, conversion optimization and CRM attribution all run under one roof, so leads convert and you can see what drove them. Reporting is transparent, the accounts are yours, and there are no punishing lock-ins.
Do you handle both B2C and B2B campaigns?
Yes. Many LA clients are B2C, including ecommerce, DTC, wellness, and real estate, where we focus on volume, efficient acquisition, and conversion-rate gains. We also run B2B programs where the goal is fewer but higher-value leads, longer consideration cycles, and tight CRM follow-up to nurture prospects to a signed deal. The strategy, creative, and measurement differ between the two, and we build the program around your sales motion. On the first call we'll map your funnel and recommend the right approach for how your Los Angeles business actually wins customers.
How do we get started?
It starts with a free strategy call. We learn your goals, your market, and your numbers, then audit your current marketing and your competitors in Los Angeles. From there we give you a clear plan and proposal with the recommended channel mix, expected timeline, and investment. To launch quickly we typically need access to your existing ad and analytics accounts, your website, and any brand assets you have. We do not guarantee number-one rankings or magic results, but we do guarantee a disciplined process and real accountability. Book a call and we will take it from there.

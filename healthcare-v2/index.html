+ The Process

The Digital Health Assessment.

Similar to how a clinic assesses a patient: goals first, then diagnostics, then a treatment plan. Our framework scores your marketing across five funnel stages and shows the path to your target.

1Step 1

In-depth questionnaire

We start with what growth means to you: a revenue number, or net new patients per month. We put it in writing, sign an NDA, and you grant access.

2Step 2

We look under the hood

We go through your accounts, Google Ads, GA4, your website, your CRM, and find out what is actually happening in there.

3Step 3

Your Digital Health Score

We assess your entire funnel for leaks: where inquiries drop off, where spend is wasted, and what fixing each gap is worth.

4Step 4

Strategy and projections

Then the plan: budgets, projections, and targets built on healthcare benchmarks, before you commit a dollar.

5Step 5

The first 90 days

Quick fixes go live in the first 30 days. The full build continues through 90. Reporting tracks against the projection from week one.

+ FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

Who is this division for? +
Clinics, multi-location groups, hospitals, associations, and health brands. If a college, a privacy act, or a platform health policy governs how you market, you are who this was built for.
Our products sell into the US. Do you cover that? +
Yes. Many Canadian health brands do most of their volume in the US. We manage both sides of the border: US ad accounts, marketplace listings, currency and delivery thresholds, and the platform health policies that differ between the two countries.
How big is your team? +
20+ as of 2026, and just like our clients, we're scaling. A mix of strategists, developers, media buyers, and creatives, all local, all in Toronto. Pop by the office on Geary and say hello.
Do you handle college advertising rules? +
Yes. Standards differ by province and profession, so creative is checked against your specific college's rules before it ever reaches your compliance officer.
How fast do we see results? +
Recovered demand shows up in weeks, because the first thing we do is find inquiries you already paid for. Paid media builds over the first quarter, and SEO over the first year.
What does it cost? +
Engagements are scoped after the strategy call, sized to your locations, channels, and how much demand we find.

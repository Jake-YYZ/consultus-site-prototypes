How we did it, step by step.

1 Step 1

Discovery

Tonic Eye Care needed to increase bookings and stand out on a limited budget against chains and well-funded independents. We dug into who actually books family eye care: parents, and especially mothers, choosing between a personal clinic and a faceless chain. The campaign objective came straight from that insight: connect emotionally with families by showcasing real stories that highlight Tonic's personalized approach to eye exams and vision therapy.

Audience researchCompetitive market scanCampaign objective
2 Step 2

UGC content creation

We produced testimonial-style videos featuring two mothers from the local community, each variation testing a different tone and storytelling angle. Authentic, unscripted stories about their children's eye exams and vision therapy, built for emotional resonance and trust. This is creative designed to feel like a recommendation from a neighbour, not an ad.

2 local creatorsTestimonial-style videoMultiple hooks per creator
3 Step 3

Statics, carousels, and targeting

In parallel, we ran single-image creatives with direct value propositions, plus carousel ads showcasing Tonic's core services: eye exams, therapy sessions, and stylish eyewear. That gave us a clean performance baseline to test UGC against. Targeting focused on women aged 30 to 55, families within a 10 km radius of the clinic, and health-conscious individuals seeking trusted, personalized eye care.

Static baselineService carousels10 km local radius
4 Step 4

A/B testing and scaling the winner

Two ad hooks went head to head, and the winning combination drove 74 percent more website conversions. Then the format test: static ads versus UGC video. The UGC won decisively, 4.52 percent click-through against 2.40 for static. We scaled the winning creative into the fall booking season, and the campaign delivered 410 leads with a 92 percent increase in overall performance.

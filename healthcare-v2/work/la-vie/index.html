+ Our Process

How we did it, step by step.

Nine weeks from signature to launch, then month one. Scroll through the build.

1 Step 1

Discovery and the Digital Growth Plan

Week zero: discovery meetings and an onboarding questionnaire digging into goals, economics, and capacity. By week two we delivered the Digital Readiness and Scaling Report, the Digital Growth Plan: projections, North Star metrics, and the channel roadmap. The clinic's existing digital foundation graded a D+. That report became the contract the whole program was measured against.

Digital Growth PlanProjections & North StarFoundation graded D+
2 Step 2

The creative engine

One production day at the clinic yielded six video concepts with two hooks each: 36 video masters and statics in five ratio sets, all inside one quarter. Two personas drove everything, united by one promise: time. Premium, calm, authority-driven creative. No fear tactics, no biohacker buzzwords. The kind of creative a physician is proud to have carry their name.

6 concepts · 2 hooks each36 video masters2 personas
3 Step 3

Channels with distinct jobs

Google captures the intent that exists, Meta builds the awareness that doesn't, and LinkedIn reaches the executives. Google Ads went live in week nine and produced two leads the same day. Search work pushed 23 keywords into the top three positions in Oakville, so the brand showed up for service-led searches where nobody knew its name yet.

Google · intentMeta · awarenessLinkedIn · executives23 top-3 keywords
4 Step 4

The CRM that closes the loop

Every lead lands in the CRM with its source attached: the campaign, the creative, the landing page. First touch to booked consult in as little as six days, and every booked assessment ties back to the ad that caused it. By week fourteen there were 512 leads on the board, Meta was producing them at $89.53 in the first ten days, and budgets were scaled by client decision.

The Practice

Portfolio as acquisition.

The renovation buyer compares portfolios for weeks before they reach out. We optimize gallery pages for Pinterest and Google image discovery, structure project case studies for AI Overview citation, and build the visual SEO surface that puts your work in front of buyers in research mode.

Long-cycle nurture.

A six-figure renovation decision takes three to nine months. We build the email and content nurture sequences that maintain presence across that window, with seasonal triggers, design inspiration, and consultation-readiness prompts that move the buyer from researching to booking.

Consultation flow tuned for high-AOV.

The quote form for a $200,000 kitchen remodel is not the same as the form for a $200 lead-gen e-book. We rebuild the consultation flow with progressive profiling, design-portfolio integration, and lead-routing logic that respects the AOV.

The Method

The Consultus Digital approach to home improvement marketing.

/01 Immerse
Audit the portfolio surface.

Portfolio pages, gallery architecture, Pinterest presence, content gaps, and the current consultation flow. We diagnose where the brand is losing buyers in research mode.

/02 Build the Foundation
Visual SEO + consultation flow.

Portfolio pages restructured for visual discovery. Pinterest and Houzz presence cleaned up. Consultation flow rebuilt for high-AOV buyers with progressive profiling.

/03 Acquire Demand
Paid + content + visual discovery.

Paid programs on Meta, Google, and Pinterest tuned for high-intent renovation buyers. Content authority built around design and project research. Long-cycle nurture catching every quote inquiry.

/04 Sell and Modify
Quarterly review tied to project revenue.

Quarterly review against booked projects, average project value, and lead-to-project conversion. Programs that produce booked work scale; programs that inflate quote volume without conversion get killed.

Why Toronto

Growth in Toronto, built on a scoreboard.

Every program we run in Toronto is measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. See the results we have driven for businesses like you.

Toronto is the economic engine of Canada and one of the most competitive business markets on the continent. The city anchors a metro of more than six million people across the GTA, runs the country's deepest concentration of financial services on Bay Street, and supports thriving real estate, professional services, ecommerce, and tech sectors. It is also one of the most diverse cities on earth, with hundreds of communities and languages shaping how people search, shop, and choose who to trust. For a local business, that mix means enormous opportunity sitting right next to relentless competition for every click.

In a market this crowded, attention is expensive and easy to waste. Toronto buyers research hard before they call, compare three or four options, and respond to brands that look credible and answer fast. The agencies competing for those same buyers are sophisticated and well funded, so spraying budget across channels without a plan is a quick way to lose money. Winning in Toronto takes sharper creative, tighter campaign structure, landing pages built to convert, and honest measurement that tells you what is actually driving revenue and what is just driving noise.

Consultus Digital approaches Toronto the way only a home team can. We are based here, we hire senior specialists here, and we run live programs for businesses across the GTA every single day. We do not bolt local keywords onto a generic template and call it a strategy. We build the whole growth engine, ads plus pages plus CRO plus CRM, so leads don't just arrive, they convert and get tracked from first click to closed deal. That is how a Toronto business turns marketing spend into pipeline it can stand behind.

FAQ

Toronto questions, answered.

Do you have a local team in Toronto?
Yes. Consultus Digital is headquartered in Toronto, at 136 Geary Avenue, Unit 109, in the west end. This is our home market and the place the entire company is built from. You work directly with senior specialists who run live campaigns across the GTA every day, not a junior team reading from a template. Being local means we understand Toronto buyers, the competition, and what it actually takes to win attention and revenue in this market. You can book a strategy call and meet the people who will run your account.
Which services should my Toronto business start with?
It depends on your goals, your margins, and how fast you need leads. If you need pipeline quickly, Google Ads and Meta Ads usually move first. If you're building durable, lower-cost visibility, SEO and AEO compound over time. Most Toronto clients end up with a blend, ads to capture demand now, SEO to own it long term, plus CRO, landing pages, and CRM so leads actually convert and get tracked. We start every engagement with an audit and recommend the mix that fits your business, not a one-size-fits-all package.
How does pricing and billing work?
We scope to the work, not a rigid menu. Pricing depends on the channels you run, the size of your ad budget, and the scope of strategy, creative, and build required. After a discovery call and audit, you get a clear proposal with the services, deliverables, and monthly investment laid out in plain English, no surprises. Ad spend is paid directly to the platforms through accounts you own, separate from our fee, so you always have full visibility into exactly where every dollar goes.
How long until I see results in the Toronto market?
Paid channels move first. Google Ads and Meta Ads can start generating leads within the first few weeks once campaigns are live and the data starts optimizing, with meaningful traction typically building over the first few months. SEO is a longer game; it usually takes several months of consistent work to climb for competitive Toronto search terms, but the results are more durable and lower cost over time. We set realistic expectations up front for each channel so you know what to watch and when, instead of being promised overnight miracles.
What reporting and transparency do I get?
You own your ad and analytics accounts, full stop. Nothing is locked behind us. You get clear monthly reporting tied to one revenue scoreboard, written in plain English, not a wall of metrics designed to look impressive. We show what is working, what we're cutting, and what each channel is contributing to pipeline. With closed-loop CRM tracking, you can see which campaign, creative, and landing page produced each lead and each closed deal. If you ever leave, everything is yours to keep.
What industries do you work with in Toronto?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also run programs in other major Toronto sectors, including real estate. The playbook adapts to your market and your buyer, but the accountability never changes: every dollar is measured against leads and revenue. If you want to know whether we've run programs for a business like yours in the GTA, just ask on your strategy call and we'll walk you through relevant work.
What makes Consultus Digital different from other Toronto agencies?
We are actually here, headquartered on Geary Avenue, and the team running your account is the team you meet. That is rarer in this market than it should be. The other half of it is that ads, landing pages, CRO and CRM sit under one roof, so leads convert and get tracked instead of leaking between four vendors who each blame the others. We report on revenue, we do not sell guarantees, and we treat your budget the way we treat our own.
Do you work with both B2B and B2C businesses?
Yes. Toronto is home to both, and we run programs for each. For B2B and professional services, the focus is high-value lead quality, longer sales cycles, and CRM-driven nurture that supports your team through a considered decision. For B2C and ecommerce, the focus shifts to creative velocity, volume, and unit economics like ROAS and MER. The strategy and channel mix change to fit the buyer, but the core discipline, measure everything against revenue, stays the same in both cases.
How do we get started?
It starts with a strategy call. We learn your goals, your market, and where you're losing opportunities today. From there we run an audit of your current marketing and accounts, then come back with a clear plan and proposal for the right channel mix. Once you're on board, the first 30 days focus on setup, tracking, and getting initial campaigns live so we start gathering real data fast. To move quickly we usually just need access to your accounts, brand assets, and a kickoff conversation. Book a call and we will take it from there.

Why Montreal

Growth in Montreal, built on a scoreboard.

Every program we run in Montreal is measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. See the results we have driven for businesses like you.

Montreal is unlike any other market in North America, and marketing here that ignores that fact gets ignored back. It is a bilingual city where a customer might search in French in the morning and English in the afternoon, where a campaign written only in English quietly loses half the room. Bill 96 and Quebec language rules aren't a footnote, they shape how you advertise, what your landing pages say, and how trust gets built. Montreal buyers can feel a translated-by-robot ad from a mile away. Consultus Digital builds for both languages as first-class citizens, not as an afterthought bolted on at the end, so your message lands the way locals expect it to.

The Montreal economy is deep and diverse, and that creates real competition for attention. The city is a global hub for AI and tech, a major ecommerce and retail center, a design and creative powerhouse, and one of the busiest tourism destinations in the country. Aerospace, gaming, fintech, healthcare, professional services and food all crowd the same feeds and search results. A Plateau boutique, a Saint-Laurent manufacturer, and a downtown law firm aren't fighting the same battle, but they are all fighting for clicks that cost money. Winning here takes channel strategy that respects the difference between a tourist, a local, and a B2B buyer doing research.

Consultus Digital approaches Montreal the way a business owner would: outcomes first, vanity metrics never. We are headquartered in Toronto with senior specialists running active campaigns for Montreal and Quebec clients, a remote-capable team, not a nameplate storefront, and we're upfront about that. What you get instead of a local sign on a door is people who actually build the ads, write the bilingual copy, optimize the funnels and read the data. We treat Montreal as its own market with its own language, culture and competitive map, then engineer the full path from ad to landing page to closed deal so the spend turns into revenue you can see.

FAQ

Montreal questions, answered.

Do you actually work with Montreal businesses, and is your team local?
Yes. We run active campaigns for Montreal and Quebec clients every day. To be straight with you, Consultus Digital is headquartered in Toronto and we serve Montreal with a remote-capable team of senior specialists, not a storefront on Sainte-Catherine. What that means in practice is the experienced people who build your ads, write your bilingual copy and read your data are the ones you work with. We know the Montreal market, the French and English buyer split, and who you compete with locally, and we build for it specifically.
Which services do you offer, and how do you decide the right mix for my business?
We cover the full funnel: Google Ads and PPC, Meta Ads on Facebook and Instagram, SEO and answer engine optimization, conversion rate optimization, web and landing page builds, and CRM with marketing automation. We do not push every service on every client. After a discovery call and audit, we recommend the mix that fits your Montreal goals, margins and timeline. A tourism brand needs a different plan than a B2B SaaS company, so we start from your numbers, not a template, and prioritize the channels most likely to drive revenue first.
How much does digital marketing in Montreal cost, and how is it billed?
It depends on scope, channels and how aggressive your growth targets are, so we scope and quote per client instead of listing a one-size price. Most engagements are a monthly retainer covering strategy, execution and reporting, with ad spend paid separately and directly to the platforms so you keep control of your money. We are transparent about what every dollar covers before you commit. On the discovery call we'll give you a realistic range for your goals so there are no surprises later.
How long until I see results, and does SEO take longer than paid ads?
Paid channels move first. Google Ads and Meta Ads can generate leads within the first weeks once tracking, creative and bilingual targeting are dialed in, with the early months spent optimizing toward efficiency. SEO is a longer game that compounds, usually taking several months to build meaningful ranking and traffic in a competitive bilingual market like Montreal. The honest answer is that paid buys speed and SEO buys durability. We often run both so you get near-term leads while building organic visibility that keeps producing after.
What reporting do I get, and will I own my ad and analytics accounts?
You own your accounts, full stop. We build campaigns inside your Google Ads, Meta and analytics properties so nothing is held hostage if we ever part ways. Reporting is monthly, in plain English, and focused on the metrics that matter: qualified leads, cost per lead, return on ad spend and revenue, not a wall of impressions. You get a clear view of what is working, what we're changing and why. We would rather you understand the numbers than be impressed by a dashboard you can't read.
What industries do you specialize in, and have you worked with businesses like mine?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also work across tourism and hospitality, tech and AI, real estate, and B2B. In Montreal specifically, ecommerce, tourism, professional services and tech are core strengths, which lines up well with the local economy. We have real case studies with verified results across these verticals. On the discovery call we'll tell you honestly whether we have relevant experience for your category and what we would do differently for the Montreal market.
What makes Consultus Digital different from other Montreal agencies?
Start with the bilingual work, because it is the part most agencies get wrong. French and English campaigns are built here as first-class versions, each written for its own audience, not translated at the last minute. From there: attribution that follows a lead from the ad to the closed deal, the full funnel under one roof so ads, landing pages, CRO and CRM answer to the same people, senior specialists doing the actual work, and accounts that stay in your name. That last one matters more than it sounds.
Do you handle both B2B and B2C, and does the approach change?
Yes, both, and the approach changes meaningfully. B2C in Montreal, think ecommerce, tourism and local services, leans on paid social, Shopping, high-volume search and fast conversion-focused pages in both languages. B2B, including the city's strong tech, SaaS and professional services scene, runs on high-intent search, longer nurture, content that earns trust with research-driven buyers, and CRM attribution that tracks a longer sales cycle. We tailor the channel mix, messaging and measurement to your buyer. A signed enterprise contract and a checkout sale are different finish lines, so we plan the route accordingly.
How do we get started, and what does the first 30 days look like?
It starts with a strategy call to understand your goals, market and current performance. From there we audit your accounts, analytics and competitors, then bring back a clear plan and recommended channel mix. To move fast we need access to your ad and analytics accounts, website, and any brand assets and offers, plus a sense of your French and English priorities. The first 30 days are setup, tracking, bilingual campaign build and launch, with early optimization. You will know exactly what we're doing and what to expect next.

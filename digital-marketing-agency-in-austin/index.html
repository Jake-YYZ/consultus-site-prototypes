Austin is one of the fastest-growing metros in the country, and that growth changes the rules of marketing here. Consultus Digital treats Austin as the living, shifting market it is, building campaigns that capture demand as it arrives rather than chasing a customer base that stopped changing years ago.

Few cities reward attention the way Austin does, and few punish a weak message faster. Consultus Digital approaches Austin the way the city approaches everything, with ambition and a refusal to settle for average. We study the local market closely, from search behavior to the events that spike competition, and we hold every campaign to a revenue standard. In a market this competitive, that integrated, accountable approach is the difference between burning budget and compounding growth.

FAQ

Austin questions, answered.

Do you have a team in Austin?
We are honest about this. Consultus Digital is headquartered in Toronto, and we do not have a physical office in Austin. What we do have is a senior, remote-capable team that serves Austin clients with overlapping working hours on Central Time. You get experienced strategists running your account, responsive communication, and the same dedicated attention you would expect from a local partner. The quality and seniority of the people on your account matters far more than a downtown address you would rarely visit anyway.
How can a Toronto-headquartered agency serve Austin well?
Digital marketing is run online, and the work happens in your ad accounts, your analytics, and your CRM, not in a conference room. Our team works hours that overlap closely with Central Time, so you reach us when you need us. We study the Austin market deeply, from local search behavior to the events and competitive dynamics that shape costs here. The result is a senior team focused entirely on your results, without the overhead and turnover that often comes with a traditional local agency. You get expertise, not geography.
Which services should an Austin business start with?
It depends on your goals, but most Austin businesses start where the fastest revenue is. If you need leads or sales quickly, paid media on Google and Meta usually moves first because it captures demand that already exists. If you want durable, compounding growth, SEO and AEO build visibility that pays off for years. We almost always pair paid traffic with conversion optimization so you aren't wasting expensive clicks. The honest answer is we recommend a starting point after we understand your business, your margins, and your competition, not before.
What is your pricing model?
We scope each engagement to the work involved, so there is no one-size-fits-all rate. Pricing depends on the services you need, the size of your ad budget, and how competitive your Austin market is. We are transparent about what you're paying for and why, with no hidden fees buried in a contract. Before you commit to anything, we walk you through exactly what is included and what results we're working toward. Book a call and we'll give you a clear, honest proposal built around your specific goals.
How long does it take to see results?
Paid media can start generating leads within the first few weeks, though the first month is usually about gathering data and finding the winning combinations. SEO and AEO take longer, typically three to six months to build real momentum, because earning durable rankings is a compounding process. CRO results build steadily as winning tests stack up. We are upfront about timelines from the start. Anyone promising overnight results in a market as competitive as Austin is either inexperienced or not being honest with you about how performance marketing actually works.
How do you handle reporting and transparency?
You always know exactly how your money is performing. We provide clear, regular reporting tied to the metrics that matter to your business, pipeline, leads, and revenue, not just impressions and clicks. You have full visibility into your own ad accounts and analytics at all times. We walk you through the numbers in plain English, explain what is working and what we're changing, and never hide behind jargon. In a market where ad costs run high, that transparency is how you make sure every Austin dollar is held accountable to real results.
What industries do you work with in Austin?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also work across other sectors driving Austin's economy, including technology and startups, music and live events, real estate, and food and beverage. Our approach adapts to each. A SaaS startup needs different campaigns than a restaurant or a real estate brokerage. What stays constant is our focus on revenue and our willingness to learn your market deeply. If you aren't sure whether we're a fit for your industry, book a call and we'll tell you honestly.
Do you work with B2B, or only B2C and ecommerce?
We work with both, and Austin gives us plenty of each. On the B2B side, we run demand generation and lead programs for tech, SaaS, professional services, and other complex sales cycles, with CRM and nurture built in to support longer buying journeys. On the B2C and ecommerce side, we drive direct response, ticket sales, foot traffic, and online purchases. The strategy changes with the audience and the sales cycle, but the discipline does not. Every program is built to drive measurable revenue, whoever your customer is.
How do we get started?
It starts with a conversation. Book a call with Consultus Digital and we'll learn about your business, your goals, and your Austin market. From there we audit your current marketing, identify the biggest opportunities, and build a clear plan with honest expectations on timelines and investment. There is no pressure and no obligation to sign anything on that first call. We would rather make sure we're the right fit before you commit. If we are, we move fast to get your program live and generating results.

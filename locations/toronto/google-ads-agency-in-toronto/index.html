FAQ

The questions Toronto businesses actually ask.

How much should I spend on Google Ads in Toronto?

Most Toronto engagements we run sit between $3,000 and $20,000 per month, with outliers in either direction. The right number depends on your average customer lifetime value, target cost per acquisition, and how saturated your category is in the Toronto market. Before we recommend a budget, we audit historical performance, current creative or content quality, and your conversion rate. Spending without that baseline is gambling, not media buying.

How long until I see results from Google Ads?

Most Google Ads engagements show meaningful signal within 14 to 28 days, with stable predictable behaviour usually settling between days 45 and 90. Anyone promising day-one outcomes on cold accounts is exaggerating. SEO is the slowest of the family. Count six months for compounding results.

Do you take over my existing account or start a new one?

In most cases we take over the existing account. Historical performance data, audience signals, and account history all carry forward, which is valuable. If your account has structural issues that cannot be fixed in place, we recommend a new build alongside the legacy account so we do not lose history.

What industries in Toronto do you specialize in?

Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. The specialization matters because Toronto buying behaviour and competitor density differ from other markets. We tune the approach to how Toronto buyers actually behave, not to a national playbook.

How is your reporting different from other Google Ads agencies?

Our standard monthly report ties every dollar of spend to a tracked outcome (lead, booking, or revenue event), broken down by campaign, channel, and audience. Most agency reports stop at impressions, clicks, and CTR. We start there and keep going, because the conversation that matters is "did this work" not "did the program run."

What does an engagement with Consultus Digital actually look like in month one?

Week one is access plus audit. Week two we lock strategy and budget. Week three we build. Week four we launch and measure. By the end of month one you have a working program against your KPIs and a baseline report. Nothing exotic, just disciplined.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. We work on month-to-month retainers. Annual commitments are available at a discount but they are optional. If our work isn't creating results, you shouldn't be locked in.

Are you a certified partner with the major platforms?

Yes. We are a certified Google Partner, Meta Business Partner, Microsoft Advertising Partner, and Zoho Authorized Partner. Partner status alone doesn't guarantee a good agency, but its absence is a fair signal that an agency may not have invested in the credentials of its team.

How much does Google Ads management cost in Toronto?

Two numbers matter: the media spend you pay Google, and the management fee you pay the agency. Most Toronto accounts we manage run $3,000 to $20,000 per month in media spend. Management fees are a flat monthly retainer scaled to account complexity, not a straight percentage of spend. Percentage pricing rewards a Google Ads management company for spending more, not for performing better. We price against the work: audit depth, build scope, and optimization cadence. Book a call and we will quote it plainly.

What does Google Ads management actually include?

Proper Google Ads management, on the platform formerly known as Google AdWords, covers the full loop: keyword and audience research, campaign and ad-group structure, ad copy testing, bidding strategy, negative-keyword lists, Shopping feed and Performance Max oversight, conversion tracking, and monthly reporting tied to revenue. Our Google Ads management services also include landing page recommendations, because the click is only half the PPC equation. If your current agency only touches bids and budgets, you're paying for monitoring, not management.

Do you manage Google Shopping and Performance Max campaigns?

Yes. For ecommerce accounts we run Shopping campaigns and Performance Max side by side: clean product feeds, tight segmentation between branded and generic queries, and negative-keyword walls that keep automated spend honest. It is the discipline you would expect from a dedicated Google Shopping ads agency in Canada, run inside your broader search program so budget shifts to whatever is converting. Ecommerce work is measured on ROAS and MER, and documented in our case studies.

Why hire a Google Ads agency instead of managing PPC in-house?

Volume of reps. An in-house marketer sees one account. A Google Ads management agency sees hundreds, across dozens of Toronto industries, so we know what a healthy CPA looks like in your category before your first dollar is spent. You also skip the hiring problem: to hire a Google Ads expert in Toronto full-time costs more than most retainers, and one person can't cover strategy, PPC execution, tracking, and reporting at once. Our clients get a senior strategist plus a dedicated Google Ads campaign specialist for less than the cost of either.

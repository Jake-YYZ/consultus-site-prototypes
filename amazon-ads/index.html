Amazon Ads, explained

What is Amazon Advertising?

Amazon advertising is one of the most flexible and essential tools for e-commerce brands. With a wide range of formats and targeting options, Amazon ads are designed to help you reach the right customers at the right time. And the best part? It works much like the PPC advertising tools you’re already using, but focused on making sure customers complete the ultimate conversion action, which is making a purchase.

Amazon ads are essentially sponsored listings. When customers search for a product on Amazon, ads push your product to the top of results, ahead of the competition. With just a subtle label reading “sponsored”, you capture attention at the very start of the journey.

Thanks to that visibility, you earn rapidly increasing impressions and clicks that lead to sales. No more competing with thousands of organic results and hoping the competition doesn’t outrank you. From exact matches to related items, Amazon advertising lets you meet customers at various points of their shopping journey, from consideration to purchase and long-term value.

Explore the formats

How does Amazon Advertising work?

For e-commerce brands chasing a solid conversion funnel, it’s impossible to ignore what Amazon advertising does for reach and impressions. It offers an advantage unlike any other: a steady stream of customers, established trust, and deep knowledge of shopping behaviors and trends.

01 / Search

Sponsored Products

The most popular type of Amazon ads. These sponsored product listings appear on shopping search results and product pages, which makes them the foundation of nearly every account.

02 / Brand

Sponsored Brands

With placement options on both desktop and mobile, you can feature your brand in shopping results complete with a custom headline, logo, and a set of products.

03 / Display

Sponsored Display

Using automation, these ads feature instantly generated creative and are targeted at relevant audiences based on their shopping interests, on and off Amazon.

04 / Storefront

Stores

For brands on the Amazon Brand Registry, Stores support multiple pages, letting you showcase best-sellers, limited-time offers, and seasonal collections with video and graphics that improve the shopping experience.

05 / Music

Audio

Play your ad on free Amazon Music accounts, reaching listeners in a format where there is no competing visual to scroll past.

06 / Streaming

Video

Play your video ad between episodes or before a movie starts on Prime Video, Amazon’s streaming platform, as well as out-stream off the Amazon network.

07 / Bespoke

Custom ads

Tailored advertising experiences created in partnership with Amazon Ads account executives, for campaigns that don’t fit the standard formats.

08 / Programmatic

Amazon DSP

Amazon’s demand-side platform for programmatic advertising, producing automatically tailored and targeted ad buys across Amazon inventory and the wider web.

09 / Measurement

Amazon Attribution

Learn how your non-Amazon ads drive shopping results on the platform, so you can see which off-Amazon spend is actually producing marketplace sales and optimize accordingly.

The advantages

What are the benefits of Amazon ads for businesses?

Amazon has built a reputation for convenience and reliable purchases among customers, and for growth at low cost among brands. Amazon ads reach people who are already shopping, and many conversions happen on the first page of results, where shoppers tend to click the first listings they see.

01 / Visibility

Search rankings

Amazon ads don’t just let you compete with the most popular and relevant products on results pages. They put your brand at the very top, edging out the competition and placing your product in front of customers who are ready to buy.

02 / Conversion

Higher conversion rates

Better than increased visibility is follow-through. By placing your product in front of the right customers, Amazon ads can deliver conversions fast, because they save people hours of browsing and comparing similar items.

03 / Automation

Increased efficiency

Amazon advertising is smart. Automated ad buying through the demand-side platform, plus campaign analytics and a large amount of data on shopping preferences and trends, makes it easier to run high-performing ads and keep them optimized.

04 / Reach

Cross-platform advertising

Products sold on Amazon are not only visible on the platform. From ranking in Google searches to appearing on social media, your Amazon listings pick up reach well beyond the marketplace itself.

05 / Value

Cost-effective ads

With pay-per-click pricing, Amazon advertising offers real value for every advertising dollar. Budgets are customizable and campaign settings are flexible. As a bonus, you don’t pay by credit card, since advertising costs are deducted directly from sales.

06 / Devices

Device friendly

Amazon ads are designed for how people actually shop, browsing and ordering quickly on whatever device is nearest. Campaign formats are tailored to devices, so you reach customers wherever they are and manage the campaigns from there too.

Practical decisions

How much does Amazon ad management cost?

Advertising on Amazon is one of the most cost-effective tools available to an e-commerce brand. You pay per click and set your own daily budget, so Amazon ads can return real value through rising sales.

But behind every successful purchase is dedicated management of those ads. That is why the management piece matters: it provides full campaign life cycle support, from audit and setup through execution, tracking, and optimization. So what does that expertise actually cost? Here is the breakdown:

01 / Fee

Management fee

Amazon ad agencies typically charge a management fee, a percentage of your monthly budget. Think of it as regular housekeeping: expert eyes on campaign performance at all times, and insight into where the opportunities for optimization and growth are.

02 / Research

Market research

Before creating ads, your agency scopes out the competition. They examine the top-performing keywords for products like yours, the search volume behind them, and your audience’s shopping preferences and behaviors, in order to build a strategy that works.

03 / Experience

Proven expertise

Amazon ad management taps into knowledge built up over years. You want to work with an experienced agency that has a strong record of successful Amazon Advertising campaigns, because that experience is what you are actually buying.

04 / Scope

Campaign strategy

Finally, the details. A turnkey solution includes campaign development, and the major cost factors are the number of ads you are running and the extent of the work involved, from audience targeting to copywriting, design, and testing new formats.

Choosing an agency

What to look for when hiring an Amazon ad management agency.

Amazon Advertising sits at the heart of an e-commerce brand, and with a huge base of shoppers ready to buy, you can’t leave your Amazon advertising to just anyone. Here is how to find an agency you can trust.

01 / Substance

Look at their website

It’s the best place to find out exactly what they offer in terms of Amazon marketing and whether it matches your needs. It is also a fair indicator of their copywriting, creative skills, and approach to keyword strategy.

02 / Evidence

Ask to see their portfolio

Learn about the campaigns they have delivered to confirm the quality of the work. A portfolio also gives you a sense of the products they have worked with and how well they know your market.

03 / History

How long they have been in business

Time in the market matters, because an agency that has managed accounts through several platform changes knows what is durable and what is a passing feature.

04 / Depth

Their knowledge of Amazon Advertising

You should be meeting their lead Amazon ad manager and creative team, so you can confirm they bring the skills a successful marketplace campaign actually needs.

05 / Commercials

Their first-month plan and fees

This should include a detailed Amazon Advertising strategy and the metrics they use to track performance. Make sure all fees are laid out properly so you aren’t met with hidden extra costs later.

06 / Reporting

Tracking, optimization, and reporting

Most importantly, ask about their approach here. They should tell you clearly how many reports you get and of what type, and be able to explain every component in detail so you have what you need to verify performance.

The wider growth system

What other services complement Amazon ad management?

Amazon ad management may be bread and butter for e-commerce brands aiming to expand reach and build long-term customer value, but it isn’t all there is. To increase your reach across the web and drive more customers to your products on Amazon, you need a comprehensive, custom-crafted digital marketing strategy.

01 / Organic

SEO strategy

Like search across the wider web, Amazon Advertising is built on a solid keyword strategy. And because sponsored product listings can surface in Google, your listings themselves need to be SEO-friendly. Combining Amazon ads with organic search improves your odds of reaching the right audiences and strengthens your organic positions at the same time.

02 / Off-platform

Paid media

Amazon ads target audiences already on the platform. But plenty of shopping journeys start with a Google search or a shopping ad on Facebook or Instagram, and you need to reach those people and direct them to your Amazon listing. Running both is similar to pairing ads with SEO, with the bonus of more visibility and less competition than organic rankings.

03 / Brand

Social media marketing

Don’t underestimate social for building visual interest and immersive brand experiences. For most retail products, a curated, active feed improves the odds that customers check out your Amazon store or buy on the social platform directly. Add the credibility of influencer marketing and you convert more followers into customers.

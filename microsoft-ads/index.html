Microsoft Ads, explained

What is Microsoft Advertising and Bing Ads?

Bing may not be at the top of your mind when it comes to PPC advertising, but its smaller search market share might be exactly what you need to capture your target audience. Microsoft’s Bing Ads platform is a smaller, direct competitor to Google Ads, working in much the same way, from targeting keywords and searches through to remarketing website visitors across the web.

Boasting a lower cost per click and a built-in audience from Bing being the default search engine on Windows devices, Bing Ads are a strong way to expand your digital marketing strategy and connect with a wider audience. And since Bing’s user demographics tend to skew older with higher household incomes, you can generate more leads, boost brand awareness, and drive sales in a less saturated search market for much less.

Here is everything you can do with Microsoft Advertising:

  • Display ads for your services, products, content, and more, to an audience you target.
  • Manage performance using ad data for constant campaign monitoring and optimization.
  • Retarget previous website visitors to keep your brand visible and broaden conversion funnels beyond your own site.
  • Control your ad spend, frequency, audience targeting, and other options for cost-effective performance.

Types of Bing Ads.

Microsoft Advertising is a dynamic tool that lets you meet potential customers exactly where they are. From basic search to multimedia, product ads, and more, Bing Ads offer a wide range of formats, each designed for a specific campaign goal.

01 / Search

Responsive search ads

The workhorse of any Microsoft Advertising account and usually the first thing to get right. Provide up to 4 sets of ad copy and 15 headlines, and Microsoft mixes and matches them into ads, then ranks by performance to push the best combinations. Expanded text ads, the old default, can no longer be created or edited, so this is where new search copy lives.

02 / Mobile

App install

A search ad with a button linked directly to the iOS App Store or Google Play, so the install happens without an extra step in between.

03 / Automated

Dynamic search

Targets niche search results by matching your site’s pages to similar products and related keywords, which surfaces demand your keyword list missed.

04 / Native

Smart search

Ads displayed in the Windows startup bar when a user starts a Bing search from there. It is a placement that exists nowhere else, and it puts you in front of someone at the moment they open their machine and start looking.

05 / Visual

Multimedia ads

Uses visuals like images and graphics to entice users to click through to a sponsored page. These cost more to produce than a text ad, so they earn their place when your offer benefits from being shown rather than described.

06 / Retail

Product ads

Built for e-commerce, boosting product listings when users search for related products, with the image, price, and merchant visible before the click.

07 / Reach

Audience ads

Native placements on articles across the Microsoft Audience Network, driven by AI targeting based on consumer intent signals such as a user’s search history and demographic data.

How the platform works

How does Microsoft Advertising work?

Microsoft Advertising taps into a smaller but capable market where customers are searching for products and services like yours. By targeting a Bing search query, your ad can be served as either an exact or broad match, letting you capture a specific audience or a wide range of customers.

Ad placement

Bing Ads do not just show up on Bing Search. Thanks to Microsoft’s reach, ads can also appear at the top, side, or bottom of AOL and Yahoo results pages. Where you land is based on:

  • How much you bid for a keyword
  • The level of bidding competition on that keyword
  • How relevant your ad is to the search query
  • The performance of your ad to date

Those four inputs behave much like the Google Ads auction, which is why an account manager who understands paid search can move between the two without relearning the fundamentals.

The Microsoft Advertising network

The platform is broader than the Bing brand suggests. One campaign can reach across:

  • Bing, Yahoo, and AOL search results
  • Microsoft’s Owned and Operated properties
  • Publisher sites in the Microsoft Audience Network
  • The Windows startup bar and Microsoft devices
  • LinkedIn profile attributes, through Microsoft’s ownership of the platform
Why Microsoft Ads matter

Why is Microsoft and Bing advertising important?

Bing advertising in Toronto can be a genuinely useful tool for certain industries and markets. Because of the lower competition and the different demographics of the typical Bing searcher, ad dollars spent here can return unusually well.

Where you might have had to spend hundreds of dollars on Google to achieve a couple of conversions, you could spend a fraction of that on Bing and get highly targeted results with strong conversion rates. The platform is also similar enough to Google Ads that it can be managed the same way, targeting demographics, geographic locations, and other factors to serve your business to the right audience.

The network is vast

Google may dominate the search market for now, but Microsoft serves ads across multiple search engines, including Bing, Yahoo, and AOL. Google remains king, and Bing Ads still present a real opportunity to reach consumers starting their shopping journey elsewhere.

Just as powerful, for much less

A less saturated market, a slightly older audience with more purchasing power, and a wide range of ad types and formats combine into a platform that is both dynamic and cost-effective. The performance is comparable; the price of entry is not.

Control and APIs

Microsoft Advertising goes beyond audience targeting and gives you APIs. That makes it easier to track campaign performance and optimize your ads programmatically rather than through the interface alone.

A low-risk way to test

Start small. Put a modest daily budget behind the searches that matter most, compare the cost per lead against your other channels, and scale only what earns its place.

Bing Ads are a powerful, understated tool, and your brand could be missing out. Even though the search volume going through Yahoo and Bing is lower than Google, there are several benefits worth weighing.

Less advertiser competition means lower CPCs, which translates directly into a lower cost per lead for your business.

02 / Audience

An older demographic

Bing and Yahoo users skew older. They are more likely to have children or buy products and services aimed at an older customer base, which is an advantage for plenty of businesses.

03 / Control

All the targeting tools and controls you need, including demographics, language, ad schedule, extensions, ad spend, and campaign optimization, available at the ad group level rather than only at campaign level.

04 / Reach

Search partner network

05 / Setup

Import from Google

Seamlessly import existing Google Ads campaigns into Microsoft Advertising for a much faster setup, keeping structure, keywords, and copy intact.

06 / Transparency

Better reporting than Google

Microsoft Advertising is more transparent about campaign performance than Google is. That includes data broken out per search partner site, so you can see exactly where your impressions came from and opt out of placements that are not converting. It is a small difference that compounds, because it turns wasted spend into something you can actually find and switch off.

Practical decisions

How much does Microsoft Advertising cost?

You already know Bing Ads cost less than the competition. The exact cost per click depends on your industry and keywords, but less competition in keyword bidding keeps prices down, and even a small investment can place your ads at the top of search.

Of course, every campaign is unique and advertising needs vary across industries. Before launching a Bing Ads campaign, find out where every marketing dollar goes:

01 / Bid

Ad budget and keyword bid

Bing Ads generally cost less than Google, so you could spend much less per click and per conversion. How much less depends on how competitive your industry is, which can drive bids up. Keep in mind that the ad auction is a constant, dynamic process, so your costs will move.

02 / Strategy

Keyword strategy and audience targeting

A key factor is the shape of your strategy: how many keywords you target, how big the audience is, and what its attributes are. The bigger the campaign, the higher the spend, so it pays to watch trends across the Microsoft Search Network and optimize by performance, device, location, and peak times.

03 / Format

Ad type

Think about the format you are using. A multimedia ad needs more production resources, since the visuals have to be designed, where a text ad is far easier to produce. For best value, find out what your audience responds to and invest in the ad types most likely to deliver.

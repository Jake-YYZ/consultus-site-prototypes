SEO and GEO in Toronto.

Search Engine Optimization is the process of convincing Google’s search engine, and your target audience, that your website is the most relevant result to show for a given query. The websites that earn those positions are called organic, natural, or earned results. They have not paid Google to appear. That earned authority is exactly what makes SEO one of the highest-ROI marketing investments a Toronto business can make.

Generative Engine Optimization, or GEO, is the newer discipline that sits alongside traditional SEO. It’s the practice of structuring your content and brand presence so that AI-powered search tools like Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini cite your business in the answers they generate. In 2026, these two disciplines are inseparable. A growing share of Google results pages now show an AI-generated answer above the organic listings, and organic click-through rates drop on the queries where those AI Overviews appear. Your business needs to be visible in both environments simultaneously.

Businesses holding page-one positions are missing the fastest-growing source of high-intent traffic if they ignore AI citations. Traffic referred from AI platforms tends to convert at a higher rate than standard organic search traffic, because those visitors have already been vetted and recommended by the AI before they arrive at your site.

SEO, explained

What is SEO in 2026, and how does it work?

SEO is the process of earning organic visibility on Google and other search engines by demonstrating that your website is the most relevant, authoritative, and trustworthy result for the queries your customers are typing.

Sites that appear organically have not paid for those positions. They earned them through technical quality, relevant and authoritative content, credible backlinks from other trusted sites, and a good user experience. The advantage of earned visibility is that it compounds: once you hold strong rankings, traffic keeps arriving without paying for every click the way you would with Google Ads.

Google’s mission has always been to deliver the most useful answer to every query, and it does that job better than anyone else by a wide margin, which is why most of the world’s search traffic runs through Google. Appearing on page one organically puts you in front of a user who has specifically asked for what you offer.

In 2026, SEO also covers everything that positions you to be cited by AI search. Google AI Overviews, which now appear on a large share of results pages, draw heavily on pages that already rank in the organic top ten. Your traditional SEO investment is the direct prerequisite for AI Overview visibility. Strong rankings are the foundation. GEO is the layer built on top.

The four pillars

On-page, off-page, technical, and local.

A complete program works on four fronts at once. Skip one and the others underperform, because Google and the AI platforms both read your site as a whole rather than as a checklist.

01 / On-page

On-page SEO

Everything on the page itself, working across three layers:

  • Content. Original, authoritative writing built around the queries your customers actually use, structured answer-first so it can be quoted.
  • HTML. Title tags, meta descriptions, headers, image alt text, and the schema markup that tells search engines and AI crawlers what the page is.
  • Architecture. Internal linking, URL structure, and a hierarchy that makes the important pages easy to find and easy to crawl.
02 / Off-page

Off-page SEO

Everything that happens away from your site to build authority:

  • Backlinks. Genuine authority development from publications, directories, and industry platforms where a citation carries real weight.
  • Citations. Consistent business listings that reinforce who you are and where you operate.
  • Brand mentions. Branded web mentions across social platforms correlate meaningfully with AI Overview appearances, so visibility off-site now feeds visibility on-site.
03 / Technical

Technical SEO

A strong technical foundation isn’t optional. It’s the infrastructure everything else is built on. Our work addresses what directly suppresses rankings: slow page speed, crawl errors, broken internal links, poor site architecture, and improperly configured robots.txt files that can inadvertently block AI crawlers like PerplexityBot alongside Googlebot.

04 / Local

Local SEO and GEO

Toronto is one of the most competitive digital markets in Canada, and the businesses winning local search are building authoritative local presences across Google Business Profile, directories, community publications, and the industry platforms AI systems draw from. Our approach covers Google Business Profile optimization, citation building, neighborhood keyword targeting, and the structured content that earns local AI citations.

Warning signs

How can you tell good SEO from bad SEO?

Three things separate an agency worth paying from one that will cost you a recovery project in eighteen months.

01 / Warning

Over-promising results

No agency can guarantee a specific ranking position or a specific AI citation outcome. Both are determined by Google’s and Perplexity’s algorithms, not by any agency. An honest agency commits to a rigorous, proven process and lets the results speak.

02 / Warning

Black-hat SEO

Buying links, hiding keywords in invisible text, and manufacturing AI citation signals through manipulative schema all deliver short-term gains and long-term penalties. Sustainable visibility comes from genuine authority built over time, not shortcuts.

03 / Warning

One-size-fits-all SEO

Every business, industry, and market is different. A strategy designed for a national e-commerce brand won’t work for a Toronto home services company. The right agency builds every program around your specific market and goals.

Transitions and oversight

Judging the work, and moving it safely.

How to tell if your SEO and GEO agency is doing a good job

  • Clear deliverables. Monthly reports covering keyword ranking progress, backlink growth, organic traffic, AI Share of Voice across major platforms, referral traffic and conversions, and the specific actions taken that month.
  • Recommendations beyond rankings. The best agencies advise on content marketing, social channel integration, internal linking, site speed, and the full range of signals that contribute to both Google rankings and AI citation frequency.
  • Improved rankings and revenue. Are you ranking for target keywords on page one? Is your brand appearing in AI-generated answers for your core queries? Are customers telling you they found you through an AI recommendation? With the right agency, all of these trend toward yes.

What happens to my SEO and GEO if I change providers?

Switching agencies carries real risks if it isn’t handled carefully. The ones to manage are a drop in keyword rankings during the transition, loss of access to historical analytics, deterioration of the link profile if the incoming agency doesn’t understand the existing backlink structure, and loss of AI visibility momentum if GEO content updates and schema maintenance aren’t handed off properly.

  1. Make sure you own your Google Analytics account and all associated data before making any changes.
  2. Document all existing backlinks, rankings, AI citation positions, and schema implementations.
  3. Secure your digital assets: hosting, email, website back-end access, and all content files.
  4. Remove the previous agency’s access before granting access to the new one.
  5. Request a full knowledge transfer including keyword targets, content calendars, link-building records, and AI visibility audit data.

Do I need GEO if I already have strong SEO rankings?

Yes, and the reason is straightforward. Organic click-through rates fall on queries where AI Overviews appear. A page-one ranking that previously generated consistent traffic now generates less if your brand isn’t also cited inside the AI Overview appearing above it, and brands cited within the AI Overview tend to earn more clicks than pages ranking below it without a citation. Strong rankings remain essential, but without GEO layered on top you’re leaving a measurable portion of the traffic and revenue those rankings should generate on the table.

Frequently asked questions

SEO and GEO, without the mystery.

Clear answers about timelines, AI search, content, collaboration, and choosing the right partner.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term investment. Traditional SEO typically produces measurable ranking improvements within three to six months and reaches its full potential at six to twelve months, depending on how competitive your industry is. Technical fixes can appear in Search Console within weeks, and GEO gains from schema and content restructuring can show within thirty to sixty days, while content authority, earned links, and AI visibility compound across quarters.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO improves visibility in traditional search engine results. GEO improves the likelihood that AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini cite your business. They share the same foundation, so Consultus Digital runs them as one connected program.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes. Consultus Digital runs SEO and GEO programs across North America, with local market programs available when a business needs city-specific search visibility.

Will my business be visible in ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews?

No agency can guarantee a citation, but GEO is designed to improve the signals AI systems use when choosing sources. That includes schema, answer-first content, crawler access, clear entities, and the authority built through traditional SEO.

Do you use AI-generated content?

We use AI for research, outlines, and keyword network mapping. Final copy is written and edited by people with subject-matter input, brand context, and a clear commercial purpose.

Can you work with our existing SEO or content team?

Yes. Consultus Digital can own the complete program or work alongside an internal team. In co-managed programs, we typically lead strategy, technical SEO, schema, measurement, and GEO while defining clear briefs and responsibilities.

How do I choose the best SEO company in Toronto?

Ask how they connect rankings to revenue, whether they earn links rather than buy them, how they measure AI visibility, and what they would do in your first ninety days. Avoid any agency promising guaranteed page-one rankings.

Do you offer managed SEO services?

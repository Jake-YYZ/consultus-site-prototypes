Why London

Growth in London, built on a scoreboard.

Every program we run in London is measured against revenue, not vanity metrics. See the results we have driven for businesses like you.

London is, by almost any measure, the most competitive marketing market in the world. It is the financial capital of Europe, home to the City and Canary Wharf, a global hub for fintech, retail, hospitality, property, creative, media, and professional services, all crammed into a few square miles of relentless demand. That density is the opportunity and the threat at once. A London buyer might be a fund manager in EC2, a founder in a Shoreditch coworking space, a tourist booking a Mayfair restaurant, or a homeowner in Richmond hiring a tradesperson. Every one of them is searching, comparing, and converting on their phone. Consultus Digital builds programmes for all of them, grounded in how London actually buys.

The flip side of all that demand is brutal competition for attention and cost per click. In London, you aren't just bidding against the firm down the road. You are bidding against national chains, venture-backed challengers, aggregators, and global brands with deep pockets, all chasing the same high-value keywords. Generic, set-and-forget campaigns quietly haemorrhage budget here faster than almost anywhere else. Winning in London takes disciplined intent targeting, tight ad-to-landing-page alignment, ruthless negative-keyword work, and creative that actually stops the scroll. Half measures get expensive fast. The businesses that treat marketing as a measured, optimised system are the ones that hold the top of the page.

Consultus Digital treats London as its own market with its own rules, not a template borrowed from elsewhere. We map the competition, the seasonality of UK demand, and the regulated realities of financial services, property, and professional services before we spend a penny. Then we run the full funnel under one roof: paid media to capture and create demand, landing pages and conversion optimisation to turn clicks into enquiries, and CRM and automation so no London lead ever slips through. We are headquartered in Toronto with senior specialists running London accounts remotely, and our working hours overlap your mornings, so you get a full bench of experts focused on your numbers, not a junior learning on your budget.

FAQ

London questions, answered.

Do you have a team based in London?
We do not have a physical office in London, and we are upfront about that. Consultus Digital is headquartered in Toronto, and we serve London clients with a senior, remote-capable team across the Atlantic. In practice that means you get a full bench of experienced specialists focused on your account, our working day overlaps your London mornings and early afternoons for calls and quick decisions, and you avoid paying the overheads of a local storefront. Everything we deliver, from strategy to reporting, is built specifically for the London market and your business.
How can a Toronto-headquartered agency serve London UK effectively?
Performance marketing runs through the same global platforms, Google, Meta, and analytics tools, wherever you sit, so what matters is the quality of the team and how well they understand your market. Our hours overlap your London mornings and early afternoons, giving us a solid window each day for calls, approvals, and rapid changes. We use shared dashboards, recorded updates, and clear reporting so you always know what is happening. Many clients find a transatlantic team an advantage: senior strategists, no local overheads, and momentum that carries on while you sleep.
Which services should a London business start with?
It depends on your goals and how quickly you need results. If you need enquiries fast, we usually start with Google Ads or Meta Ads paired with a dedicated landing page and conversion tracking, so you can see results within weeks. If you're building durable, lower-cost demand, SEO and content are the long game worth starting early. Most London businesses benefit from a blend: paid media for immediate pipeline while SEO compounds underneath. We recommend a starting point based on your market, margins, and competition, then expand the funnel as the numbers justify it.
How does your pricing work?
We scope every engagement to the work involved instead of selling a fixed package, because a Canary Wharf fintech and a single-location London restaurant have very different needs. Typically there is a monthly management fee based on the channels and scope we run, separate from the ad spend you pay directly to Google and Meta. We are transparent about exactly what your fee covers and where every pound goes. Before we start, you get a clear proposal with scope, deliverables, and pricing, so there are no surprises and you can judge the return against the investment.
How long until we see results?
Paid channels move fastest. With Google Ads or Meta Ads and a solid landing page, most London clients see meaningful enquiry data within the first few weeks, then we optimise from there as the data matures. SEO is a longer game: expect a few months to build momentum and compound, depending on how competitive your terms are, and London terms are competitive. Conversion optimisation improves results across every channel over time. We set realistic milestones at the start and report against them honestly, so you always know whether the programme is on track.
How transparent is your reporting?
Completely. You get clear, regular reporting tied to the metrics that matter to your business, booked calls, qualified enquiries, cost per lead, and revenue, not a wall of vanity numbers. We walk you through what is working, what we're testing, and what we're changing next, in plain English. You retain ownership of your ad accounts, analytics, and data throughout, so nothing is locked behind us. Our goal is that you always understand exactly what your London marketing is doing and what it is earning.
What industries do you work with in London?
Consultus Digital runs four divisions: Healthcare, Trades & Construction, DTC & eCommerce, and Professional Services. We also work across other London sectors, including hospitality and tourism, property and real estate, and creative and media. Our approach adapts to each: regulated, compliance-aware campaigns for finance and property, conversion and feed-driven work for retail, and high-intent lead generation for professional services. What stays constant is the method. We learn how your specific London buyers search and decide, then build a measurable programme around capturing that demand and turning it into revenue.
Do you handle B2B as well as B2C and ecommerce?
Yes. We run both, and the strategy shifts with the audience. For B2C and ecommerce, we lean on Shopping, Meta creative, local search, and aggressive conversion optimisation to drive volume and protect margins in a crowded London market. For B2B, think City and Canary Wharf firms, we focus on precise intent targeting, LinkedIn and search where it fits, longer nurture sequences, and CRM-driven follow-up, because the sales cycle is longer and each lead is worth more. In both cases we tie spend back to revenue, so you can see exactly what is working.
How do you handle GDPR and UK data requirements, and how do we get started?
We take UK data rules seriously and build campaigns to respect them, working within UK GDPR and PECR around consent, cookies, and tracking, with compliant consent management and analytics setups. We coordinate with your team or legal advisers on consent banners, privacy notices, and data handling so your stack is set up properly. Getting started is simple: book a call with us. We learn your goals, audit your current marketing and who you're up against, then come back with a clear, compliant plan and pricing for the London market before you commit to anything.

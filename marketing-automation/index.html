Marketing service

Marketing Automation and Email.

Work smarter while steadily building customer engagement and trust. Marketing automation is the practice of automating parts of your marketing strategy through software configured to handle the repetitive, time-consuming work.

It’s used for prospecting, lead nurturing, cross-selling, upselling, and customer service. From chatbots and email campaigns to webinar promotion, it works as a standalone service and does considerably more when combined with the rest of a strategy.

Automation frees you from time-intensive processes. By automating customer touchpoints and the collection of insight, you keep the sales cycle moving without redundant work. You don’t have to automate the whole journey at once: start with the flows that save your team the most time, then add more as the results come in.

Email sits at the center of most of this. With billions of people checking their inbox every day, it remains one of the most powerful channels available to a business of any size, and the only one where the customer has explicitly invited you into a space reserved for people they want to hear from.

How it works

How does marketing automation work?

Automation is the backbone of efficient workflow. With competing priorities, the smart move is to focus on your strengths and delegate the rest to a properly configured platform. Six things happen when you do.

01 / Nurture

Build and nurture relationships

Send personalized emails to prospective and current customers, tailored to their preferences and to where they actually are in the journey rather than to when your newsletter happens to go out.

02 / Pipeline

Strengthen the sales pipeline

Follow up with leads and send abandoned cart reminders. These are the interactions that move stalled leads along the funnel and turn intent that was already there into realized revenue.

03 / Insight

Track the customer journey

Gain real insight into how buying happens: how long it takes customers to complete a purchase, and which assets they engaged with before they decided.

04 / Reporting

Automated reports

Rich visualizations and analysis of aggregated interactions give you a full picture of leads, conversions, and sales per period, without anyone assembling it by hand.

05 / Service

Improve the customer experience

Tools like chatbots let you communicate with customers and guide them along the funnel, which lowers bounce rate, captures essential data, and lets the sales team spend time where it counts.

06 / Integration

Connected to your CRM

Most automation is integrated with the CRM, which is what lets marketers and salespeople work from the same record. Without that connection you get two systems telling you different things about the same customer.

Automation, explained

What is marketing automation?

Marketing automation is the process of taking manual, time-consuming tasks and putting them on autopilot. Most solutions integrate with a company’s CRM so marketers and salespeople can work more efficiently from shared data.

By analyzing your sales cycle, your customer data, and what you actually sell, a specialist can program a solution that fits your business. The program lets you send personalized content that promotes the right product, at the right time, to the right customer.

It can improve nearly every part of the sales cycle: lead generation, lead management, closing, and revenue growth.

Lead scoring sharpens lead management. The platform adds points for the actions that signal buying intent, such as opening emails, clicking through to pricing, or returning to the site, and passes a lead to sales once it crosses a threshold you set. Your team calls the people most ready to buy first.

Email marketing is the channel most of that runs through. It’s the promotion of your brand and offerings through direct messaging to defined audiences in your database, people who signed up for a newsletter, created an account, or otherwise gave consent. Unlike social advertising, you send a specific message to specific people who have already shown interest, and most tools support enough personalization that each recipient feels the email was written for them.

Why it matters

Why is marketing automation important?

It helps you work better, and frees you to focus on product or service delivery. Businesses that use it report streamlined marketing and sales, stronger customer engagement, and better lead quality. They also spend less time on manual tasks, generate more leads, and get their data into one place.

Audience / 01

Grow your email subscribers

Automated sends earn more opens and clicks than one-off newsletters, because each message lands when it is relevant to the reader. The most popular automated emails are welcome emails, thank-you emails, and ongoing engagement sends.

Commerce / 02

Automated checkout

For e-commerce, an automated checkout makes sure customers complete purchases immediately and stay satisfied with the journey. Orders get received and processed without a person in the loop slowing it down.

Data / 03

Real-time updates and insight

Modern platforms give you a live snapshot of marketing and sales performance, with alerts for new subscribers, form submissions, and other interactions, so the data you act on is current rather than last month’s.

Some worry that automated email alienates customers. The opposite tends to be true. Because automation gives you insight into where each customer actually is, it enables the kind of personalization that a manual send never achieves at scale.

The channel

What are the benefits of email marketing?

Amid rapidly refreshing social feeds, email is very much alive. There’s something deeply personal about a message delivered straight to an inbox, and email marketing starts from the fact that the customer invited you there.

01 / Personal

Personalized customer experience

Email is the pinnacle of personalization, and many B2B marketers name newsletters as their best way to nurture leads. Segmentation lets you send by genuine interest rather than to everyone at once.

02 / Return

Higher return than social ads

Welcome emails are among the most opened messages a business sends, and they earn far more opens and clicks than a standard promotional email. Email also tends to deliver more conversions and a higher order value than social, thanks to that personalization.

03 / Systems

Automation for conversions

With the right strategy and platform, email runs like clockwork. Set up segments and tailor content for regular automated touchpoints, and the whole sales cycle becomes something you manage rather than operate.

04 / Traffic

Drive traffic

The best newsletters link back to your site, directing customers to products, services, and exclusive offers, and prompting them to revisit abandoned carts. Most B2B marketers count email among their top organic distribution channels.

05 / Reach

Accessibility and credibility

Email reaches any device, and much of it is now read on a phone. It’s also permission-based: customers consented to hear from you, so a professional, branded template builds credibility and recall rather than interrupting.
